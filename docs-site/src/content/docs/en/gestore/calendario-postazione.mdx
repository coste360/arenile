**Where:** Operations → **Map** → spot sheet → **Calendar**. **Who:** whoever sees the map; customer names only with
the permission to see bookings.

The calendar shows the whole season of a spot: one row per month, one column per day. Each day is split in half,
**morning on the left and afternoon on the right**, with the colour and glyph of the status (free, booked, season
pass, released, held, blocked, to collect).

<Screenshot name="admin-spot-calendar" alt="Season calendar of a spot with the booked days" />

- **A free day** starts a new booking from that day, with the following free days already proposed (up to 14).
- **A booked day** opens its booking.
- On a computer, hovering the **Calendar** button shows a preview.

## Range view

On the map, **Range** instead of **Day** shows a whole period: a spot free only in part shows **free in part N/M**
(free days out of the total). Tap it to see the free days and start the booking or go to
[Find a spot](/en/gestore/trova-posto/).

**Where:** Configuration → **Zones**. **Who:** owners and managers.

A **zone** groups spots or equipment sold in the same way: "Front row", "Gazebos", "Pool", "Free sunbeds". For each
zone you decide **how** it is sold, **at what price** (category) and **how much** can be sold online.

## The three assignment modes

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Chosen spot" icon="approve-check">
    The customer picks **the exact spot** on the map. The classic case of numbered rows.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Zone" icon="puzzle">
    The customer picks **the zone**; the spot is assigned later, by staff or automatically. Ideal for premium areas or
    to fill rows compactly.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Quantity" icon="list-format">
    Only **a number of items**, without position (for example free sunbeds): availability is a counter on a
    [pool](/en/gestore/pool/). A quantity zone has no spots on the map.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

You can combine the modes in the same establishment: the public page adapts by itself and shows the map only if at
least one zone visible online is chosen-spot, zone cards for "Zone" zones and the quantity selector for quantity
zones.

## Creating or editing a zone

<Steps>

1. Press **New zone** (or the pencil of an existing zone).
2. **Code** (lowercase letters, digits, hyphens or underscores) and **Name** in the enabled languages.
3. **Assignment mode**: **Chosen spot**, **Zone** or **Quantity**.
4. **Map**: the map holding the zone's spots (not needed for quantity zones).
5. **Price category**: the key used by price rules, for example `row1`. Several zones can share a category.
6. "Zone" mode only: **Automatic assignment** (see below).
7. **Visible on the public page**: untick it to sell the zone at the desk only.
8. **Online quota**: **No limit**, **Percentage** or **Number**, and the **Quota value**.

</Steps>

In the list each zone shows its mode, map, number of spots and an online sales label: **All online**, **Online
30%**, **Online max 20** or **Desk only**.

## Automatic assignment (Zone mode)

A zone in **Zone** mode sells **capacity**: a counter equal to the number of active spots, created and kept in sync
by the system. The booking line is created **to assign** and the spot is chosen:

- **automatically on confirmation**, if the zone has **Automatic assignment** (online paid bookings too: the
  assignment happens in the same operation as the confirmation);
- **by hand** by staff: the [Today](/en/gestore/oggi/) page lists the lines **To assign** and the booking detail has
  the **Assign** button on each pending line.

The automatic algorithm sorts the lines from the longest to the shortest (the hardest to place) and for each one
picks, among the spots free on **all** dates, the one with the best score: continuity with the same customer's
previous booking, closeness to the other lines of the same booking, customer preferences and compact filling of the
rows. If no spot is free for the whole period, the line stays to assign and staff place it by hand (splitting over
several spots is allowed only if the policy allows a spot change during the stay).

<Aside type="note" title="Guarantee">
  In a "Zone" zone the sum of assigned and unassigned lines never exceeds the active spots, for every date and slot:
  you never sell more than you can place.
</Aside>

## Online quotas

The quota limits how much of a zone can be sold **online** (and through the **API**), for each date and slot. The
**desk ignores the quota**: staff can always sell anything free.

| Type | Example | Available online |
|---|---|---|
| **No limit** | — | everything free |
| **Percentage** | 30% of 40 spots | at most 12 sold online, minus those already sold online that day |
| **Number** | 20 | at most 20 sold online |

As a formula: `available online = min(total free, quota − already sold online)`. When the quota is used up the
customer sees "No more online spots for these dates", while the zone remains sellable at the desk.

<Aside type="tip">
  Quotas keep places for regulars who arrive without booking, or for tomorrow's pass holders. Starting with a cautious
  quota in August and widening it as you watch the trend is a good strategy.
</Aside>

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I change a zone from "Chosen spot" to "Quantity"?**
A quantity zone cannot have spots on the map: first move or delete its spots.

**Why can't I delete a zone?**
Because it is used by price rules, promo codes, agreements, packs or policies: the error lists what to change first.

Knowing the customer journey helps you answer the phone and configure the page well. It is built for a smartphone,
even inside the beach's website: **a complete booking takes less than two minutes**. No account, no fees.

## The spot hold

When the customer moves from the "What" step to "Details and payment", the spot (or the quantity) is **held for
them**: nobody else can take it, neither online nor at the desk. The hold lasts `ARENILE_HOLD_TTL` (10 minutes by
default) and is extended during payment up to a maximum of 30 minutes. If it expires the spot becomes free again and
the customer sees *The time to complete the booking is over: please choose again*. In the panel that spot appears as
**Pending** while the hold is active.

## Payment and confirmation

<Steps>

1. With Stripe the page says *Secure payment with Stripe: you will be redirected and then brought back here* and opens
   the payment page at full window, even when embedded in your website. If the host website prevents it, the buttons
   **Open the payment page** and **Open in a new tab** appear.
2. On return the page shows *Waiting for the payment confirmation…* (*Do not close the page: it usually takes a few
   seconds*) until Stripe tells Arenile the outcome.
3. Then **Booking confirmed** appears with the **Booking code**, the **QR code to show at the entrance**, the details
   (paid, **Still to pay**, spots **Being assigned** for zones with automatic assignment) and the **Add to calendar**
   button (`.ics` file). The customer receives the email with the manage link.

</Steps>

| Outcome | What the customer sees |
|---|---|
| Payment failed or abandoned | **Payment failed** / **Payment not completed**: the spot is still held and they can **Try the payment again** |
| Approval required | **Request sent**: the beach will reply shortly; you find it **Awaiting approval** in the panel |
| Hold expired | **Booking expired**: the spot has been released |
| Rejected by the manager | **Request not accepted** |
| Slow confirmation | *You will receive an email as soon as the payment is confirmed* (reconciliation recovers lost webhooks) |

## The statuses the customer goes through

<StatusBadges />

| Status | For the customer |
|---|---|
| **Held** | Has picked the spot and is filling in the form: spot held |
| **Pending payment** | Is on Stripe: hold extended |
| **Awaiting approval** | Request sent, awaiting your decision |
| **Confirmed** | Paid (or confirmed without online payment) |
| **Expired** | The hold expired before payment |

From the link in the email the customer opens **Your booking** and can:

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="View and show" icon="document">Status, **Booked by**, details, QR code to show at the entrance, **Documents** (QR and calendar file), link expiry.</Card>
  <Card title="Pay the balance" icon="approve-check">If only a deposit was paid, pay the rest online.</Card>
  <Card title="Change" icon="pencil">**Change dates** within the policy deadline: **Calculate the new price** shows the new total, the difference to pay or in their favour and any change fee.</Card>
  <Card title="Cancel" icon="close">**See the refund** shows refund or credit per the accepted terms; where allowed they choose **Refund to the payment method** or **Credit for a future booking**.</Card>
  <Card title="Release days" icon="random">For pass holders, if the policy allows it: they pick the days, **Calculate the credit** and release them; the spot goes back on sale.</Card>
  <Card title="Request a new link" icon="email">With the email used to book and the booking code, if the link expired or was lost.</Card>
</CardGrid>

If the policy does not allow changes, the area says so: *For changes please contact the beach directly*.

<Aside type="note" title="Link privacy">
  If the link is invalid or expired the page only says *The link is invalid or has expired*. Requesting a new link
  always answers *If the details match a booking, you will receive a new link by email*: nobody can find out whether
  an email address has booked.
</Aside>

## If something goes wrong

| The customer sees | What happened |
|---|---|
| *The spot has just been booked by someone else.* | Another customer or the desk took it a moment earlier: they pick another spot |
| *No more online spots for these dates.* | Online quota used up (the zone may still be free at the desk) |
| *Quantity not available for these dates.* | Pool exhausted |
| *The request does not meet the beach's booking rules.* | Policy: lead time, duration, start day… |
| *The payment service is not responding: try again shortly.* | Stripe temporarily unreachable |
| *Booking session not found: please start again.* | They closed the tab or the session expired |
| *Too many requests: please wait a few seconds.* | Per-IP request limit |

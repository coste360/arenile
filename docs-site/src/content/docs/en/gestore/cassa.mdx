**Where:** Operations → **Cash desk**. **Who:** cashiers, managers and owners operate; other roles with access to
reports browse the history read-only.

Arenile's cash desk records what comes in and goes out during the day, by method, and at closing compares the count
with the expected amount. It is for management control and reconciliation with the fiscal register, POS and Stripe;
**it does not replace tax obligations** (see [Daily takings](/en/gestore/corrispettivi/)).

<Screenshot name="admin-cash" alt="Cash desk with the open session, totals by method, takings and movements" />

## Opening the cash desk

<Steps>

1. Count the cash in the drawer.
2. Enter the **Opening float** and press **Open session**.

</Steps>

There is one open session per register (**Main register**). Without an open session cash payments cannot be recorded
(*The cash desk is closed: open it to record cash payments*).

## During the day

The session panel shows **Opening float**, **Total takings**, **Cash in − cash out**, **Expected cash in drawer**
(float + cash takings + cash in − cash out) and **Takings by method**.

- **Take payment**: find the booking by code or customer; the proposed amount is the **Balance due**. Choose the
  method and **Record payment**.
- **Quick sale**: opens the [daily sale](/en/gestore/vendita-giornaliera/) for walk-in customers (full day, half
  day or entry, with a numbered ticket). Cash and card takings of tickets go into the open session like the others; a
  voided ticket with a refund shows as an outflow.
- **Movement**: **Cash in** or **Cash out**, always with a **Reason** (change, bank deposit…).
- Payments recorded elsewhere (booking detail, spot sheet) while the session is open end up in the same session.
  **Counter sales** without a booking appear as such.

The **Takings** and **Movements** tabs list everything that happened in the session.

<Aside type="note" title="What about online payments?">
  Stripe payments go to the Stripe account and are recorded on the booking with the online method: they do not pass
  through the drawer and do not change the expected cash.
</Aside>

## Closing the cash desk

<Steps>

1. **Close cash desk**.
2. For each method Arenile shows the **Expected** amount: enter the **Counted** one.
3. The **Difference** is computed by itself: **Balanced**, **Surplus** or **Shortfall**. Add a note if needed (for
   example the reason for the difference).
4. **Close session**.

</Steps>

After closing, **Print Z report** produces the printable summary: register, opening and closing, float, cash in, cash
out, takings by method, notes and number of transactions. During the day **Print summary** prints the current
situation.

## Session history

**Session history** lists the sessions with opening, closing and status. **Session detail** reopens the closing
summary, takings and movements of a past session.

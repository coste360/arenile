**Where:** day **Map** (computer and phone), **Today** → **To do**, **Today's tickets**. **Who:** cashiers, managers
and owners.

When there is a queue at the desk it pays to sell **only the zone** ("Front row") and pick the umbrella later. The
sale takes a place of the zone at once, so you never sell more tickets than places; the ticket stays **To assign**
until you put it on a spot.

<Aside type="note" title="Seat zones and zone zones">
  In zones sold **by zone** the place comes out of the zone capacity. In zones sold **by seat** Arenile provisionally
  holds the last free spot of the zone: on the map you see it taken by the ticket number until you choose the right
  one.
</Aside>

## Assigning with a tap

<Screenshot name="admin-assign-panel" alt="Day map with the To assign panel and the zone's tickets" />

<Steps>

1. Open the day **Map**: the **To assign** panel lists the tickets in number order (#037 …).
2. Tap a ticket: the free spots of its zone are highlighted.
3. Tap a free spot of the zone. Arenile confirms *Ticket #037 assigned to spot A4*.

</Steps>

If another register took that spot in the meantime, Arenile says so (*The spot was just taken: choose another one*)
and the ticket stays to assign: the database checks it, not the browser.

## Automatic assignment

**Assign automatically** places the tickets to assign, in number order, on the free spots of their zone with the same
rule as the automatic assignment of zone bookings (see [Zones](/en/gestore/zone/)). Tickets that find no place stay in
the panel (*2 tickets assigned, 1 without a free spot*).

## Today and Today's tickets

- **Today** → **To do** → **To assign** also lists the zone tickets, with the number and **Assign on the map**.
- In **Today's tickets** the **Assign** button opens the spot choice; for an assigned ticket **Move** puts it on another
  free spot (also later in the day).

## Map labels

Spots taken by a ticket show the **last name** given at the sale or, without a name, the ticket **number** (**#037**).
The **Labels** switch (Code / Customer / Both) applies to tickets too, as to other bookings (see
[Day map](/en/gestore/mappa-del-giorno/)).

<Aside type="caution" title="Different price category">
  If you move a ticket to a spot of another price category, the booking total is recalculated but the amount printed
  on the ticket stays the one collected: settle any difference at the cash desk.
</Aside>

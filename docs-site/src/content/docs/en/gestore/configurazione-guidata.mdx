**Where:** Configuration → **Guided setup**. **Who:** owners and managers.

The guided setup takes a new establishment from empty to ready to sell. It opens by itself after an
establishment is created and can be reopened at any time. A bar at the top shows the progress ("N of 6 steps
completed or skipped"); on the left the list of steps with their state **to do**, **completed** or **skipped**.

<Screenshot name="admin-setup-wizard" alt="Guided setup with the list of the six steps and the current step" />

## The six steps

<Steps>

1. **Details and languages.** Check name, languages and contacts: they appear on the public page and in emails to
   customers. The same fields are in [Establishment](/en/gestore/stabilimento/).

2. **Template.** Pick the template closest to your establishment and press **Use this template**. Each card shows
   the layout, zones, packs and periods that will be created.

   <Tabs>
     <TabItem label="Classic lido with rows">
       Umbrellas in numbered rows, priced by row and period. Zones **Front row**, **Second row**, **Back rows** and
       **Gazebos**; packs **Basic** (1 umbrella and 2 sunbeds), **Comfort** (1 umbrella, 1 sunbed, 1 deckchair) and
       **Gazebo** (subject to approval); extras extra sunbed, changing cabin, beach towel, parking; policies for
       online, desk and gazebos. The most common case.
     </TabItem>
     <TabItem label="Equipped beach by quantity">
       No map: customers choose how many umbrellas and sunbeds, availability is a counter (pools of 80 umbrellas
       and 200 sunbeds, editable).
     </TabItem>
     <TabItem label="Lido with pool">
       Rows on the beach plus a pool area with sunbeds, in zone mode with automatic assignment.
     </TabItem>
     <TabItem label="Empty">
       Nothing prefilled: you configure everything by hand from the menu items.
     </TabItem>
   </Tabs>

3. **Map rows.** Set **Rows** and **Spots per row** (the total updates as you type) and the dates of the **First
   season** (**Year**, **Opening**, **Closing**). **Create season and map** generates everything in one operation
   and shows the summary: spots, zones, packs, periods. If the establishment already has a season or a map, the
   step tells you and you can continue or go to the editor.

4. **Quick price list.** A **Category** × period grid with daily prices including VAT (for example `25.00`).
   Leave the cells you do not need empty and press **Generate price rules**: the base rules are created (replacing
   those generated before). Half days and long formulas are configured later in [Prices](/en/gestore/prezzi/).

5. **Public page.** Turn on online bookings, choose the colours and copy the code to paste into your website.
   Everything is fine-tuned in [Public page and widget](/en/gestore/pagina-pubblica/).

6. **Payments.** **At the desk** (cash, card terminal, bank transfer) is ready straight away, with nothing to set
   up. To take payment for online bookings, **Set up online payments** opens the page where you connect your
   Stripe account: you can also do it later, see [Online payments with Stripe](/en/gestore/pagamenti-online/).

</Steps>

**Skip this step** moves on without saving; **Back** returns to the previous step. **Finish setup** shows the
summary, the skipped steps and the shortcuts **Edit the map**, **Review prices**, **Establishment settings** and
**Reopen the guided setup**.

<Aside type="tip" title="Goal: under 15 minutes">
  With the "Classic lido with rows" template, 8 rows of 15 spots and the quick price list, a complete establishment
  is configured in under a quarter of an hour. Then fine-tune at leisure.
</Aside>

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I run the guided setup again on an establishment already configured?**
Yes, but the steps that create data (template, map rows) recognise that a season or a map already exists and do
not duplicate them. The quick price list instead replaces the rules it generated before.

**Where do the categories of the quick price list come from?**
From the price categories of the zones (and any overrides on single spots). If none exist, the step tells you:
create zones with a category in [Zones](/en/gestore/zone/).

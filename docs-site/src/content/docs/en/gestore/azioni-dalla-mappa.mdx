**Where:** Operations → **Map**. **Who:** whoever creates bookings (owner, manager, cashier) for group bookings;
whoever runs the beach operations (check-in and moves permission) for blocks.

From the [day map](/en/gestore/mappa-del-giorno/) you can select several spots together and, in one go, turn them
into **one group booking** or **block** them.

<Screenshot name="admin-map-selection" alt="Day map with three spots selected and the action bar" />

## Selecting several spots

- Press **Select**, then tap the free spots one by one.
- Or drag a **rectangle** on the map (with the mouse or a finger): every free spot inside it is selected. With two
  fingers you still pan and zoom the map.
- With keyboard and mouse: **Shift**-click selects the range from the last chosen spot, **Ctrl**-click (**Cmd** on a
  Mac) adds or removes a spot, even without pressing **Select** first.

Only spots **bookable in the slot shown** can be selected; if a colleague books one meanwhile it leaves the
selection by itself. The bar at the bottom shows the count and the actions: **Group booking**, **Block**, **Clear**
and **Exit selection**.

## Group booking

<Steps>

1. Select the spots and press **Group booking**.
2. The new booking sheet opens with all the spots: choose pack, customer, dates and slot as for a normal booking.
   The quote is computed from the price list for every spot.
3. **Create booking**.

</Steps>

The result is **a single booking** with one line per spot: one code, one balance, one manage link for the
customer. It is **all or nothing**: if even one spot is no longer free nothing is created and the panel tells you
which one.

## Blocking spots

<Screenshot name="admin-map-block" alt="Block 2 spots dialog with period, slot and reason" />

<Steps>

1. Select the spots and press **Block** (for a single spot: **Block spot** from its sheet).
2. Choose the **Period**, the **Slot** (full day, morning or afternoon) and optionally the **Reason** (for example
   *umbrella maintenance*).
3. **Block**.

</Steps>

Blocked spots cannot be booked, neither at the desk nor online, and appear grey as **Blocked** on the map. Blocking
several spots is all or nothing. To remove a block open the spot sheet and press **Unblock**: the block is removed
for its whole period.

<Aside type="note" title="Concurrent changes">
Map actions (check-in, no-show, cancellation, move) always send the version of the booking they read: if a
colleague changed it meanwhile the panel tells you instead of overwriting.
</Aside>

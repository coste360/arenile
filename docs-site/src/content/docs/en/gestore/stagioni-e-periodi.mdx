**Where:** Configuration → **Seasons and periods**. **Who:** owners and managers.

- The **season** is the opening period of a year (for example from 1 May to 30 September).
- **Rate periods** split the season into price bands: low, mid, high season, peak (Ferragosto) or free codes. Price
  rules use them as a condition.
- **Local holidays** add to the Italian national holidays already included.

## Creating a season

<Steps>

1. Press **New season**.
2. Enter **Year**, **Opens on**, **Closes on**.
3. Choose the **Status**: **Draft** (in preparation), **Active** (on sale) or **Closed**.
4. Save.

</Steps>

The public page limits its calendar to the active season. Off season the map of the day shows "Establishment
closed". There is one season per year.

## Rate periods

Each day of the season belongs to **at most one period**: Arenile rejects a range that overlaps another period or
that falls outside the season's dates.

<Steps>

1. In the season press **New period**, or use the **Typical periods** (**Low season**, **Mid season**, **High
   season**, **Peak**) to start from a ready-made code.
2. **Code**: `low`, `mid`, `high`, `peak` or a free code (for example `ferragosto`).
3. **Colour**: the colour of the period in the timeline and in the public calendar (if you enable highlighting
   periods).
4. **Date ranges**: one or more **From**/**To** ranges (**Add range**). A period can have separate ranges, for
   example "mid" in June and in the first half of September.

</Steps>

The **Period view** switches between **Timeline** and **Calendar**. If some days of the season are not covered by
any period, a notice counts them: rules tied to periods will not apply to those days.

<Aside type="caution">
  Deleting a period means the rules that use it no longer apply on those days: check the
  [coverage check](/en/gestore/prezzi/#coverage-check) afterwards.
</Aside>

## Local holidays

Days treated as holidays by price rules with the "Holidays" condition, in addition to the national holidays already
included in the program.

- **Specific dates**: one precise day (**Add date**), for example an event.
- **Yearly holidays**: `MM-DD` format (**Add yearly holiday**), for example `08-16` for the patron saint.

## Copying the season to the next year

Copying the price list (periods, rules, fixed prices, policies) is done from
[Prices → Copy season](/en/gestore/prezzi/#season-copy): dates shift keeping the weekday where it makes sense and the
target season is created as a draft.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I have two seasons in the same year?**
One season per year. For a winter reopening use periods and [closures](/en/gestore/chiusure/).

**Must Ferragosto be a period of its own?**
It is not required: you can make one `peak` period for all of August and a rule with a specific date range for
14–16 August. Choose whichever is clearest for you.

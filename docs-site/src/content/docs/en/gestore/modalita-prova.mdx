**Where:** Configuration → **Test mode**. **Who:** owner and manager switch it on and off; all staff see the notice
while it is on.

**Test mode** lets you try Arenile before opening (or train the staff) without distorting the figures: every
booking and payment created while it is on is a **test** row and stays out of reports, the "Today" indicators,
corrispettivi, the Z closing and exports. At the end you delete them all with one click.

<Screenshot name="admin-test-mode" alt="Test mode page with the switch, the preview link and the test bookings" />

## Switching it on and off

<Steps>

1. Turn on **Test mode on**. The yellow notice **Test mode is on** appears at the top of every panel page: whoever
   is working knows they are creating test data.
2. Try what you need: desk bookings, bookings from the map, cash payments, the public page from the preview link.
3. Turn the switch off before working with real customers.

</Steps>

The flag is written on the booking when it is created: switching test mode off does not turn test bookings into
real ones, and switching it on does not touch real ones. A payment inherits the flag of its booking. Test bookings
take spots like any other until you delete them.

## Public page: only from the preview link

While test mode is on the public page **does not accept bookings from customers**: whoever tries reads that online
bookings are temporarily suspended. To try it yourself:

<Steps>

1. In **Public page preview** press **Create preview link**.
2. **Copy** the link or **Open** it: it is valid for **2 hours**, on another device too (your phone, to walk
   through the customer journey).

</Steps>

The option **Test bookings from customers too** opens the page to everybody: not recommended when the page is
already published, because real customers' bookings would be test rows (excluded from the figures and deletable).

## Online payments with test keys

If you saved the Stripe **test** configuration too in [Online payments](/en/gestore/pagamenti-online/), test mode
uses it automatically: no real charges, Stripe test cards. Without test keys online payments are disabled during
the test (payment at the venue).

## Deleting test bookings

<Steps>

1. In **Test bookings** check the count.
2. **Delete test bookings**: the confirmation shows how many bookings and payments will be deleted.
3. **Delete**.

</Steps>

The deletion is final and happens in a single operation: bookings, allocations, payments, documents and related
messages. **Real bookings are never touched** and the activity log stays. Customer credit generated by the tests is
removed and the balance recomputed.

<Aside type="note" title="Webhooks and integrations">
Events of test bookings are not sent to [webhooks](/en/gestore/chiavi-api-e-webhook/): connected systems do not see
the tests.
</Aside>

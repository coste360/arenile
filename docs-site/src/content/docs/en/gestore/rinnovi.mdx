**Where:** Operations → **Renewals**. **Who:** cashiers, managers and owners.

After the [season rollover](/en/gestore/cambio-stagione/), a **renewal campaign** offers every pass holder of the
past season the **same spot** in the new season, at the new price list. Until the pre-emption deadline the spot is
reserved for the holder: nobody can book it, neither at the desk nor online.

<Screenshot name="admin-renewals" alt="Renewals page: campaign summary with countdown and table of proposals with status and sending" />

## Creating the campaign

<Steps>

1. **New campaign** (or **Offer renewals** at the end of the season rollover).
2. Choose the **Source season** and the **New season**.
3. Set the **Pre-emption deadline**: until that day (at 23:59, establishment time) the spot stays reserved. It must
   be before the new season opens.
4. Optional: **Name** and **Internal notes**.
5. **Preview**: see the proposals with their amount and the **Excluded passes** with the reason (spot unavailable or
   deactivated, no price in the new list, already renewed, anonymised customer).
6. **Create proposals**.

</Steps>

Each proposal creates a held booking in the new season: on the map the spot shows as **Held for renewal**. No
email is sent yet.

A pass that covered the whole season renews for the whole new season; a shorter one keeps the same dates moved by
one year.

## Sending the proposals

- **Bulk actions** → **Send to those who have not received it**: every holder with an email gets the proposal with
  the personal link.
- **Bulk actions** → **Remind those who have not replied**: sends the link again to those who have not answered.
- From the row menu: **Send the proposal** or **Send a reminder** to one person.

The **Sending** column tells whether and how many times the proposal went out; customers without an email are
marked **no email** and are handled at the desk.

## Answers

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Waiting** | open proposal, spot reserved |
| **Accepted, to be paid** | the customer accepted online and is paying |
| **Renewed** | booking confirmed in the new season |
| **Declined** | the customer gave up: spot released |
| **Expired** | no answer by the deadline: spot released |

### At the desk

- **Accept (at the desk)**: with **Confirm now** the renewal is confirmed and the balance is recorded at the
  [cash desk](/en/gestore/cassa/) like any booking; without it, it waits for the payment.
- **Decline and release**: closes the proposal (with an optional **Reason**) and puts the spot back on sale.

The same actions are available on the selected proposals, together with **Extend the deadline** and **Release
now**.

### Online

The customer opens the link of the email and sees the proposal in the manage area: new and previous price,
deadline, choice between deposit and full amount (following the [policy](/en/gestore/policy/)), use of the
available credit and acceptance of the terms. **Renew and pay** leads to the online payment; **I will not renew**
releases the spot.

<Aside type="note" title="Confirmed only by the payment">
An online renewal becomes **Renewed** only when the payment confirmation arrives from the provider (or the manual
driver), never from the browser return. Without online payments, the acceptance waits and you confirm it at the
desk.
</Aside>

## Deadline and closing

- At the deadline the proposals without an answer become **Expired** and the spots are bookable again
  automatically, within a minute.
- **Change deadline** moves the deadline of the proposals still open (their bookings stay reserved until the new
  date).
- **Close campaign** closes all open proposals at once and releases their spots.
- **Bulk actions** → **Release expired pre-emptions** forces the release without waiting for the automatic check.

When a customer asks for their data to be erased (GDPR), the decline reason they wrote is wiped with the other free
texts.

**Where:** Operations → **Reports**. **Who:** owners, managers and read-only users (for example the accountant).
Other roles see **Access not allowed**.

The **Reports** page answers the end-of-day and end-of-season questions: how full the beach was, how much you
collected, where bookings come from, how many pass holders released days and who still has to pay. Every report has
cards with the key figures, a chart and a table with the same data, and can be exported to CSV.

<Screenshot name="admin-reports" alt="Reports page: period selection, summary cards with occupancy, collected and bookings" />

## Choosing the period

At the top there is a single bar with the **Period**, valid for every report:

- **Season**: choose the season from the list. It is the starting choice (the current season, otherwise the
  latest one opened).
- **Month**: choose **Month** and **Year**.
- **Custom**: choose **From – to** on the calendar, at most 400 days.

Below the bar the panel shows the exact range (*Data from … to … (inclusive)*). All dates are in the
establishment time zone.

## The reports

Choose the report from the tabs at the top.

| Report | What it shows | Group by |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Summary** | The **Period indicators**: **Occupancy**, **Collected**, **Booked**, **Bookings**, **Average booking value**, **Cancellations**, **No-shows**, **Season passes sold**, **Open balances**. | — |
| **Occupancy** | Slots occupied over the capacity of open days, with pass holders, released and blocked. | **Day**, **Zone**, **Row**, **Time slot** |
| **Revenue** | Gross, taxable, VAT and refunds. | **Day**, **Week**, **Month**, **Channel**, **Pack**, **Extra**, **Payment method**, **VAT rate** |
| **Bookings** | Count, share, value, average value and average lead time. | **Channel**, **Lead time**, **Duration**, **Day** |
| **Cancellations and no-shows** | Cancelled and no-shows over the total, lost and refunded value. | **Day**, **Month**, **Channel** |
| **Season passes and releases** | Passes sold and renewals, released and resold slots, resale revenue and credits issued. | **Day**, **Month**, **Zone** |
| **Top customers** | Ranking with bookings, spot-days, value and last booking. **Sort by** value or number of bookings; **Customers** chooses how many to show (10, 20, 50, 100). | — |
| **Open balances** | Bookings of the period with an amount still to collect: code, customer, status, dates, total, paid and balance. | — |

A **slot** is one spot for half a day (morning or afternoon): occupancy counts the occupied slots over the available
ones, excluding closure days and blocked spots.

### Collected or booked

The **Revenue** report has a **Basis**:

- **Collected**: successful payments in the period, net of refunds. It is the cash basis and matches to the cent
  the collected amount of the [daily takings](/en/gestore/corrispettivi/) of the same period.
- **Booked**: the value of the bookings for the days of the period, regardless of payment.

Some groupings exist for one basis only: **Payment method** only with **Collected**, **Pack** and **Extra** only
with **Booked**.

<Aside type="note" title="Which date a figure belongs to">
Under every summary card the panel prints the date basis: **By first day of stay** (a booking sold in March for
August counts in August) or **By payment date**. The **Bookings** report instead counts by sale date, because it
measures how and when you sell: this is why, over the same period, the two figures can differ.
</Aside>

## Comparison with the previous year

<Steps>

1. Turn on **Compare with …** in the period bar (the year is the one before the chosen period).
2. In the **Summary** the cards (except **Open balances**) show the previous year's value and the change; below
   appears the **Comparison with …** section with the **Indicators compared with the previous year** and the trend
   over time.
3. Choose the **Alignment**: **Same calendar dates** (15 August with 15 August) or **Same weekday** (Saturdays with
   Saturdays, useful for weekends).

</Steps>

When the previous year's value is zero the percentage change cannot be computed and the card is marked **New**.

## Exporting to CSV or Excel

Every report has the **Export CSV** and **Export Excel** buttons, which download exactly what you see: chosen
period, grouping and basis. The comparison with the previous year can be exported too. The CSV uses the Italian
format (`;` separator, decimal comma); the Excel file (`.xlsx`) has the same columns, with real numbers and dates.

<Screenshot name="admin-report-exports" alt="Reports with the Export lists menu open" />

**Export lists**, next to the period, downloads the full lists as Excel or CSV: **Customers**, **Bookings in the
period**, **Season passes** and **Payments in the period**. The columns are the same as the CSV files, with no extra
personal data, and [test](/en/gestore/modalita-prova/) bookings are always excluded.

<Aside type="tip" title="For the accountant">
Create a user with the **Read only** role (see [Team and roles](/en/gestore/team-e-ruoli/)): they can view and
export the reports without being able to change anything.
</Aside>

The **Top customers** ranking shows only first and last name: email and phone stay in the
[customer record](/en/gestore/clienti/), also in the CSV.

**Where:** Operations → **Calendar**. **Who:** everyone views; cashiers, managers and owners edit.

The planning shows **spots as rows** (grouped by zone and row) and **days as columns**: the right view to place long
stays without leaving one-day gaps.

<Screenshot name="admin-planning" alt="Planning calendar with spots as rows, days as columns and bookings as bars" />

## Navigating

The bar at the top: days earlier and later, **Today**, **From** and **Visible days** (up to 62). The start date stays
in the page address: you can bookmark it or share it with a colleague.

Each booking is a bar on its spot; empty cells are free, closure days are marked as closed.

## Creating a booking from the planning

<Steps>

1. Select a range of free cells on the same spot (by dragging, or with <kbd>Shift</kbd>+arrows).
2. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> or **New booking**: the sheet opens with spot and dates already filled in.

</Steps>

## Moving a booking

Drag the bar onto another spot or other dates (or, with the keyboard, <kbd>M</kbd> on the booking under the cursor,
arrows and <kbd>Enter</kbd>). Arenile shows the **Change booking…?** preview with origin and destination spot, new
dates, conflicts and price difference; **Confirm move** applies it.

<Aside type="note">
  If a colleague changed the booking in the meantime, **Booking changed by another user** appears: **Reload** and
  recompute the move. No change is ever silently overwritten.
</Aside>

## Keyboard

| Keys | Action |
|---|---|
| Arrows | Move between cells |
| <kbd>Shift</kbd>+arrows | Select |
| <kbd>Enter</kbd> | Open the booking or create one on the selection |
| <kbd>M</kbd> | Move the booking under the cursor |
| <kbd>Esc</kbd> | Cancel |

**Where:** Operations → **Bookings**. **Who:** everyone views; cashiers, managers and owners create and edit; refunds
belong to managers and owners.

## List and filters

<Screenshot name="admin-bookings" alt="Booking list with filters for period, status, channel and spot" />

Filter by **Search** (code, customer or phone), **Period**, **Status**, **Channel** (Desk, Online, API), **Spot**,
**Type** (bookings and/or season passes) and **Only with balance due**. The columns show **Code**, **Customer**,
**Period**, **Spots**, **Status**, **Channel**, **Total** and **Balance**. **Clear filters** starts over.

## Booking statuses

<StatusBadges />

```text
          ┌──────────── Expired (hold expired)
          │
Draft ─▶ Held ─▶ Pending payment ─▶ Confirmed ─▶ Checked in ─▶ Completed
                     │                  │
                     │                  ├──▶ Cancelled (with refund or credit)
                     │                  └──▶ No-show
                     └──▶ Awaiting approval ─▶ Confirmed | Rejected
```

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Held** | Resources held temporarily (online checkout or booking in progress) |
| **Pending payment** | Online payment started; hold extended until the outcome (max 30 minutes) |
| **Awaiting approval** | Resources held pending your decision |
| **Confirmed** | Resources occupied for good |
| **Checked in** | Check-in recorded |
| **Completed** | Closed automatically at the end of the stay |
| **Cancelled**, **No-show**, **Expired**, **Rejected** | Final statuses: resources are released |

Every status change goes through a single point of the program and writes, in the same operation, the **activity
log** and the matching event: the booking's **History** is always complete.

## New booking

<Screenshot name="admin-booking-new" alt="New booking sheet with mini-map, customer and price preview" />

<Steps>

1. Press **New booking** (from the list, the map, the planning or the customer record).
2. **When**: **Period**, **Time slot** (Full day, Morning, Afternoon) and **Formula**.
3. **What and where**: choose the **Pack** and then **Spots on the map** (tap the free spots on the mini-map; the
   price is *per spot*) or **Zone or quantity**. Add **Extras**.
4. **Customer**: **Find customer** by name, phone or email, or **New customer** (last name and at least one contact).
   If a customer with the same email or phone exists, Arenile suggests them (*Possibly already registered*).
5. Check the **Price preview** (breakdown, discounts, VAT, deposit) and the **Terms applied** (payment, balance,
   cancellation).
6. **Payment and notes**: **Take payment now** with amount and method, or leave the balance due (at the desk or on
   arrival). Add **Internal notes**.
7. **Create booking**.

</Steps>

If the request does not comply with the [policies](/en/gestore/policy/), the preview explains why. You can still go
ahead with **Book anyway (overrides the booking rules, recorded in the activity log)**.

<Aside type="tip">
  If taking the payment fails (for example the cash desk is closed and the method is cash), the booking is still
  created and the message tells you: record the payment from the detail after opening the cash desk.
</Aside>

## The booking detail

<Screenshot name="admin-booking-detail" alt="Booking detail with lines, allocations, payments, notes and history" />

At the top: **Total**, **Paid**, **Balance due**, **Deposit due** and, if held, how long the hold lasts. Below:

- **Lines**: pack, spot (or **To assign**), extras, total; **Move** or **Assign** actions.
- **Allocations**: the occupied half-days, by date, slot and resource (spot or pool unit).
- **Payments**: payments and refunds with method and outcome.
- **Notes**: **Internal** or **For the customer**.
- **History**: who did what and when (**Operator**, **Customer**, **System**, **API integration**).

### Status actions

| Action | When | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| **Confirm** | Held booking | Manual confirmation: the spots stay booked |
| **Approve** / **Reject** | Awaiting approval | Confirmed, or spots released and customer notified |
| **Check in** | Only on the days of the stay | The customer is marked as present |
| **No-show** | From the first day of the stay | The spots of future days are released |
| **Change dates** | Confirmed | New dates with a new price |
| **Cancel booking** | Confirmed | Spots released, refund per the terms |

### Changing dates

**Change dates** re-prices the booking with the new dates and re-allocates the spots in the same operation. The
**Effect of the change** shows **Current total**, **New total** and the difference **To collect** or **To refund**, or
the unavailable dates. If the terms limit changes, you can override with **Override the modification limits
(recorded)**.

### Moving a line

**Move** on a line: choose the **New spot** among the free ones, **From** (empty = the whole stay), **Check** and
**Move**. The price is recalculated if the category changes.

### Payments

**Record payment**: the proposed amount is the balance; choose the **Method** (Cash, Card, Bank transfer, Wallet,
Customer credit, Other). Cash payments require an open [cash session](/en/gestore/cassa/). A held booking paid at the
desk up to the required deposit is confirmed in the same operation.

### Refunds

**Refund** records a refund, never more than what was paid: **Amount**, **Form** (money or credit to the customer),
**Reason**. For a payment made online with Stripe you can refund **through Stripe**: the money goes back to the
customer's card and the refund stays **Pending** until Stripe confirms it (see
[Online payments](/en/gestore/pagamenti-online/#refunds)).

### Automatic refunds

When a booking paid online is cancelled (by the customer with the manage link or from the panel) and the terms
grant a refund to the card, Arenile **reserves the refund in the same operation as the cancellation**: it cannot be
lost, even if the payment provider does not answer at that moment, and it is never executed twice.

The **Automatic refunds** section of the detail shows every refund with its status: **Waiting to be sent**,
**Waiting for confirmation** (sent, waiting for the provider to confirm), **Refunded**, **Failed**. Arenile retries
by itself with growing waits (from 1 minute to 12 hours, up to `ARENILE_REFUND_MAX_ATTEMPTS` attempts, default 10).
When the attempts run out the refund becomes **Failed**, the owner gets a [notification](/en/gestore/notifiche/)
and **Retry** queues it again. The customer sees the refund status in the manage link.

### Cancelling

<Steps>

1. **Cancel booking**: the spots are released.
2. The **Refund preview** shows **Paid**, the **Percentage from terms** and the amount **Per terms**, computed on the
   terms accepted at booking time.
3. Choose the **Settlement**: **As per terms**, **Refund another amount**, **Credit to the customer** or **No refund**.
4. If it is due to bad weather, tick **Cancelled for bad weather**: the policy's weather protection applies.
5. Enter the **Reason** and confirm.

</Steps>

### Email and manage link

From the detail you can **resend the confirmation email** and **regenerate the customer's manage link** (see
[Email and notifications](/en/gestore/email/#the-manage-link-magic-link)).

## Online bookings

Bookings from the public page have the **Online** channel and appear in the panel as soon as they are created (held,
with the hold countdown). While the customer fills in the form, the booking is tied to an internal placeholder
customer, which never appears in lists: at the details step it is linked to a new customer with the typed details.
The public page never links a booking to an existing customer with the same email or phone (whoever knows a
contact detail could read their data): duplicates are spotted from the customer card.

<Aside type="caution">
  Do not confirm by hand an online booking "pending payment" because the customer on the phone says they paid: wait
  for Stripe's confirmation (it arrives by itself, or through reconciliation) or record the payment yourself at the
  desk if the customer pays there.
</Aside>

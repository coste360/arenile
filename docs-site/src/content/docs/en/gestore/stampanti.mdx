**Where:** Configuration → **Printers**. **Who:** managers and owners configure printers and routing;
cashiers see the status, check the connection and send the test print.

Arenile prints **non-fiscal payment receipts**, **cash closings (Z)** and **[daily sale](/en/gestore/vendita-giornaliera/)
tickets** straight to the ESC/POS thermal printers on the beach network, with no print dialog, with paper cut and
cash drawer opening.

<Aside type="caution" title="Available with the desktop app">
ESC/POS printing works when Arenile runs on the establishment's computer: with the [desktop
app](/en/gestore/app-desktop/), LAN mode included. The server must reach the printer on the beach network,
which an online server (VPS or Docker) cannot do. On a server installation the Printers page explains this (the
entry is not in the Configuration menu): the cash closing prints **from the browser**, as always, while the
non-fiscal receipt exists only on the thermal printer.
</Aside>

## Supported printers

Any **ESC/POS** thermal printer with a **network** interface (Ethernet or Wi-Fi) that accepts direct printing
on TCP port **9100** (RAW, also called JetDirect or AppSocket). We designed it for:

| Brand | Typical models | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Epson | TM-T20II/III, TM-T88V/VI/VII, TM-m30 | ESC/POS reference; CP858 character table = 19 |
| Xprinter | XP-58, XP-80, XP-Q200 (LAN/Wi-Fi versions) | Some models use different table numbers |
| Rongta | RP80, RP326, RP850 (LAN/Wi-Fi) | Epson compatible |
| Bixolon | SRP-330, SRP-350, SRP-E300 | Epson compatible |
| Others | "Generic ESC/POS" 58 or 80 mm printers | Try the test print |

<Aside type="note" title="Not in this version">
**USB** or **Bluetooth** printers and printing from an online installation through a bridge on the cash desk
computer are not supported yet: in those cases you print from the browser.
</Aside>

## Connecting the printer

<Steps>

1. Connect the printer to the beach network (Ethernet cable to the router, or Wi-Fi with the maker's utility).
2. Find its **IP address**: hold **FEED** while switching it on to print the self-test page, or look at the
   router's device list. Give it a **fixed IP** in the router so the address never changes.
3. In Arenile open **Configuration → Printers** and press **Add printer**.
4. Enter **Name** (e.g. *Bar till*), **IP address or host name** and **Port** (almost always 9100).
5. Choose the **Paper width** (58 or 80 mm): **Characters per line** follows (32 or 48).
6. Press **Save**, then **Check**: the status becomes **Reachable**.
7. Press **Test print**: out comes a page with the establishment name, the printer address, accented letters,
   the € sign, the styles, a QR code and the cut.

</Steps>

Only **local network addresses** are accepted (192.168.x.x, 10.x.x.x, 172.16-31.x.x): a public address is
refused, even a host name that points outside the network. The same goes for the computer's own address
(127.0.0.1) and for cloud service addresses: the printer is another device. Developers testing with a virtual
printer on the same PC set `ARENILE_PRINTERS_ALLOW_LOOPBACK=true`.

## Printer options

- **Character table**: **CP858** (default) prints € and accented letters on almost every printer. If odd
  symbols come out instead of *à è é*, try another **Table number (ESC t)** from the manual. Characters the
  table lacks are replaced (*€* becomes *EUR* on CP437).
- **Paper cut**: full, partial or none; **Feed lines** before the cut.
- **Print the logo**: the establishment logo converted to black and white (dithered) for thermal paper.
- **Cash drawer**: if the drawer is connected to the printer (RJ11 cable), choose the **pin** (2 is the
  standard). It opens at the end of the print when the routing rule says so.
- **Active**: an inactive printer receives no prints.

## Routing: which printer for which document

In **Routing** choose, for each document, the printer and the number of **Copies**:

- a rule for **All registers** is the default;
- a rule for one **register** wins over the default (e.g. the bar prints on its own printer);
- **Open drawer** opens the drawer at the end of the print (printers with a drawer only, once even when the
  print is resent);
- with **Copies** 2 or 3, the second copy onwards carries a **COPY** mark at the top.

Without a rule the closing and the daily ticket print from the browser and the receipt is not offered. Press **Save routing** to
apply the changes.

## Printing from the cash desk

In [Cash desk](/en/gestore/cassa/) the **Print summary** and **Print Z report** buttons use the thermal
printer when there is a rule and the printer is active and reachable: *Sent to Bar till* appears. Otherwise the
browser print opens with a notice explaining why (no printer configured, printer inactive or unreachable). After
a **Take payment**, if a receipt printer is ready, the confirmation offers **Print receipt** (a **non-fiscal**
receipt).

## Printing daily sale tickets

With a **Daily ticket** rule and the printer ready, the ticket of the **Quick sale** (automatic print after the
sale, Umbrellas and Entries tabs) and the **Reprint** from **Today's tickets** go to the thermal printer: *Sent to
Bar till* appears. The ticket shows the number (**#037**), the day, the zone and the spot or *to be assigned* (entry
tickets list their ticket types), formula, pack and extras, total with the VAT breakdown, payment method, the QR
code (the same as on the browser ticket, read at the gate), the line **Not valid for tax purposes** and, on
reprints, **REPRINT**; the customer name is not printed. With **Open drawer** the drawer opens only for tickets paid
in cash. Without a ready printer the ticket prints from the browser as before, in the chosen format.

## Latest prints and retries

**Latest prints** lists every print with its outcome. If the printer is off or offline, Arenile retries on its
own after 5 seconds, 15 seconds, 1 minute and 5 minutes; then the print is **Failed** and you can press
**Retry**. While one printer is not answering, queued prints for the other printers still go out. A double
click on the same button creates one print; pressing **Print summary** again later is a deliberate reprint and
creates another. If Arenile is closed right while sending a print, it resends it on restart (it may come out
twice, without opening the drawer again). The content of finished prints is deleted after 30 days; only the row
with the outcome remains.

## Troubleshooting

- **Unreachable**: check that the printer is on, on the same network as the computer running Arenile, and that
  its IP has not changed (self-test page).
- **Wrong letters**: change **Table number (ESC t)** or **Character table**.
- **Text too wide or too narrow**: fix **Characters per line** (32 on 58 mm, 48 on 80 mm; some 80 mm printers
  use 42).
- **The drawer does not open**: try the other pin and check that the rule has **Open drawer**.

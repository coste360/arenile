**Where:** the whole panel, on any device. **Who:** every user.

On the beach the connection sometimes drops. The manager panel keeps working in a limited way: you can view the map
and the bookings of today and tomorrow, check customers in, add notes and take cash payments. What you do without a
connection is sent by itself as soon as the network is back.

## Installing the panel as an app

The panel can be installed like an app: it opens full screen from its icon, without the address bar, on the
**Today** page.

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="Android">
    Open the panel with Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu and choose **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**).
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="iPhone and iPad">
    Open the panel with Safari, tap **Share** and then **Add to Home Screen**.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Computer">
    With Chrome or Edge open the panel and use the install icon in the address bar (or the browser menu →
    **Install**). With Safari on a Mac: **File** menu → **Add to Dock**.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

The panel address is the same one you use in the browser and must be `https`. On a computer you can also use the
[desktop app](/en/gestore/app-desktop/), which brings Arenile straight onto the cash desk PC.

### Updates

When a new version comes out, **A new version of the panel is available** appears with **Update** and **Later**.
The update never starts by itself, so nobody is interrupted during a check-in; queued operations are not lost when
updating. The first time **The panel is ready to work offline too** also appears.

## What works without a connection

The indicator at the top always shows the status: **Online**, **Offline** or **Syncing**, with the number of
operations **queued** and of those **to review**. Without a connection the **You are offline** notice appears with
the time of the data you are viewing.

| Offline you can | Offline you cannot |
| --- | --- |
| View the map and the bookings of today and tomorrow | Create new bookings |
| See the balances to collect of the day | Move a booking to another spot |
| Check in a booking | See the takings of the day (*Not available offline*) |
| Add a note to a booking (**Add note**) | Refunds, card payments or payment links |
| Record a cash payment on an existing booking (**Cash payment**) | Change configuration, prices and users |

Creating and moving bookings needs the connection: only the server guarantees that the spot is free and that nobody
sells it twice. The panel explains it in the form (**Not available offline**) and sends nothing.

<Aside type="caution" title="Data must be saved first">
The panel prepares the offline copy while online and keeps it fresh as long as it stays open. Open the panel on the
device at least once a day with the connection: when no data is saved the notice says *There is no saved data of
this establishment on this device*.
</Aside>

## The operation queue

Check-ins, notes and payments made offline show up on the map at once and go into the queue. When the connection is
back they are sent **in order**, each **only once**, even if the network drops halfway. The typical arrival
(collecting the balance and then checking in the same booking) works offline too.

Tap the indicator to open **Offline operations**: you see what is queued, with the time, and you can use **Send
now**.

If the server refuses an operation (for example a cash payment without an open [cash session](/en/gestore/cassa/),
or a check-in on a booking cancelled in the meantime) **Offline operations to review** appears:

<Steps>

1. Tap **Review** to see the operation and the reason.
2. Fix the cause (for example open the cash desk) and choose **Retry**, or **Discard** if the operation is no longer
   needed.

</Steps>

If the session has expired you see *Session expired: sign in again to send the queued operations*.

## Which data stays on the device

To work offline the panel saves in the browser, only for the establishment you are viewing: your profile, the maps,
the beach situation of today and tomorrow (customer name, booking code and balance) and the details of the bookings
of these two days that you opened. It never saves the customer records, contact details, payments, reports or staff
users.

- Saved data expires after 48 hours and is replaced when you switch establishment.
- With **Sign out** the panel tries to send the queued operations and then deletes the saved data; the same happens
  when another user signs in on the device.
- **Queued operations** are never discarded by themselves: they contain cash really collected and check-ins really
  done. If a colleague signs in on the same device, they see them in the indicator and they are sent with their
  session.

<Aside type="tip" title="Shared devices">
On the tablet or phone that passes from hand to hand among the staff, always sign out at the end of the shift.
</Aside>

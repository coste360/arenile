**Where:** Operations → **Today** (also the panel's home page). **Who:** every role; actions depend on permissions.

The morning page: the live status of the beach and the list of things to do. It refreshes by itself when a colleague
checks someone in or a customer books online.

<Screenshot name="admin-today" alt="Today page with key figures, spots-by-status chart, map of the day and to-do lists" />

## Key figures of the day

| Figure | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Occupancy** | Percentage of occupied spots (e.g. "92 of 124 spots occupied") |
| **Arrivals** | Bookings starting today, with the number of departures |
| **Pending check-ins** | Arrivals not yet checked in, with how many are already present |
| **Pass holders expected** | Pass holders occupying their spot today |
| **Released spots** | Pass days put back on sale today |
| **Takings today** | Total collected, with the **Open balances** |

The **Spots by status** chart uses the same colours as the map.

## Map of the day

A compact version of the [operational map](/en/gestore/mappa-del-giorno/) for today: tap a spot to open its sheet.
The **Map**, **Planning** and **Cash desk** shortcuts lead to the full pages.

## To do

Three lists, each with its counter:

- **Check-in**: who arrives today and is not checked in yet. The button next to the name records the arrival with one
  tap.
- **Balances**: today's bookings with a balance to collect. Open the booking or go to the cash desk.
- **To assign**: lines of "Zone" mode zones without a spot yet. Open them and use **Assign** (see
  [Zones](/en/gestore/zone/#automatic-assignment-zone-mode)).

<Aside type="tip" title="The morning round">
  Before opening: empty **To assign**, check the **Pass holders expected** and the released spots, open the
  [cash desk](/en/gestore/cassa/). During the day the **Check-in** list empties by itself as staff record arrivals,
  even from their phone.
</Aside>

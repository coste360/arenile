## Producing the summary

<Steps>

1. Choose the **Period**: **Day** (with the **Date**) or **Month** (with **Month** and **Year**).
2. Read the **Total takings**, then the **By VAT rate** table (**Rate**, **Taxable**, **Tax**, **Total**) and the
   **By payment method** table (**Transactions**, **Total**).
3. With several [registers](/en/gestore/casse/) choose **All registers** or a single one: for one register only the
   payments recorded in its sessions count (online payments stay in the all-registers total).
4. **Download CSV** (ready for an Italian Excel: `;` separator, decimal comma, Italian headers), **Download Excel**
   (`.xlsx`) or **Download JSON**.

</Steps>

Amounts **include VAT**, split by rate from the price breakdown stored with each booking; refunds are negative. The
total always equals the period's takings.

## What Arenile does and does not do

<Aside type="caution">
  **Fiscal compliance of the setup remains the responsibility of the operator and their accountant.** Arenile is a
  management tool: it does not provide tax advice.
</Aside>

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Is Arenile a fiscal register (*registratore telematico*)? | **No.** It neither stores nor transmits takings to the Italian Revenue Agency. |
| Does it issue the commercial document (*documento commerciale*)? | **No**, today: it is issued with your fiscal register or the procedure you already use. |
| Do its receipts and summaries have fiscal value? | **No**, they are non-fiscal documents. |
| Does it help with daily takings? | **Yes**: the summary by rate and method, to hand to the accountant. |
| Are VAT rates preset? | **No**: you set them, per establishment, pack and extra. |

The fiscal module is built around a driver interface: the default driver issues no documents (you use your register),
and drivers for the families of fiscal registers may arrive as contributions (see
[Extensions](/en/sviluppo/estensioni/)).

## Online payments

With Stripe, online takings go to the establishment's Stripe account and appear in the daily takings with the
matching method. The tax and accounting treatment of online deposits, balances and refunds must be agreed with the
accountant.

**Where:** Configuration → **Devices**. **Who:** every user pairs and revokes their own devices; the owner sees
those of the whole team, can pair one for a colleague and revoke any of them.

A **paired device** is an Arenile app that connects to your Arenile with a personal access, without entering the
address and credentials every time: today it is the [desktop app](/en/gestore/app-desktop/) in remote mode, later
also the mobile app for staff <Badge text="Coming soon" variant="caution" />.

<Aside type="note" title="The browser needs no pairing">
To use the panel from a phone, a tablet or another PC just open it in the browser and sign in with your own user:
no pairing is needed. You can also [install it as an app](/en/gestore/uso-offline/).
</Aside>

## Pairing a device

<Steps>

1. **Pair a device**.
2. Owner only: in **Device of** choose **Myself** or the colleague who will use it, then **Generate the QR code**.
   The device gets the permissions of that user's role, in this establishment only.
3. The QR code appears with the **Pairing code** (eight characters, for example `ABCD-EFGH`) and the address of
   your Arenile, with the **Expires in** countdown.
4. On the device open the Arenile app and scan the QR code, or type the address and the code by hand (in the
   desktop app: **Pair this PC** window, fields **Instance address** and **Pairing code**, then **Pair**).
5. The panel shows **Device paired**: **Done**.

</Steps>

The code works **only once** and **expires after 5 minutes**: if it expires (**Code expired**) use **Generate a new
code**.

<Aside type="caution" title="Desktop app in local mode">
If you run Arenile with the desktop app on the cash desk PC, other devices can reach it only with the **local
network mode** on: while it is off the page shows **Turn on the local network mode** and pairing is not available.
See [Desktop app and local network](/en/gestore/app-desktop/#local-network-mode).
</Aside>

## The list of devices

For every device you see the name and the system (**iPhone / iPad**, **Android**, **Windows**, **macOS**,
**Linux**), the **User** with their role, **Last used** and the **Status**:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Active** | The device can access. |
| **Fiscal bridge** | It is the cash desk PC that forwards fiscal documents to the connected fiscal printer (desktop app in remote mode). |
| **Revoked** | It can no longer access; it appears only with **Show revoked**. |

The device you are using is marked **This device**.

## Access security

- Every device has **its own access**, different from the others: revoking one does not affect the others.
- The access works **only for this establishment**, even if the user works in several establishments of the same
  company, and only with the permissions of their role.
- If the user is deactivated or loses their role on the establishment, the device stops working.
- A device **not used for 90 days** loses its access by itself: it must be paired again.

## Revoking a device

<Steps>

1. In the device row choose **Revoke**.
2. Confirm: the device loses access immediately. To use it again it must be paired with a new code.

</Steps>

Revoke right away a lost or stolen phone, the PC of a collaborator who no longer works with you or a device you do
not recognise.

<Aside type="caution" title="Revoking the fiscal bridge">
If you revoke the device acting as **Fiscal bridge**, fiscal documents stay pending until you register another
bridge (by pairing the cash desk desktop app again).
</Aside>

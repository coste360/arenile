**Where:** Configuration → **Registers** (management), Operations → **Cash desk** (sessions). **Who:** owner and
manager create and edit registers; whoever runs the cash desk chooses which one to open.

Every establishment has the **Main register** (*Cassa principale*), created automatically. If you take payments in
several places (entrance, bar, beach rentals) add a register for each: every register has **its own session**,
float, Z closing and totals in the corrispettivi.

<Screenshot name="admin-cash-registers" alt="List of registers with the default one and the session status" />

## Managing registers

<Steps>

1. **New register**: enter the **Name** (for example *Bar*). The **Code** is optional: when empty it is derived
   from the name and cannot be changed afterwards.
2. **Order** sets the position in the list and in the selector of the Cash desk page.
3. To stop using it turn **Active** off: close its open session first.

</Steps>

- The **default** register is always active and cannot be deleted.
- A register with sessions in its history can only be **deactivated**; only a never-used register can be deleted.
- Changes to registers go to the activity log.

## Opening a register

On the [Cash desk](/en/gestore/cassa/) page the **Register** selector shows every active register with its status
(*open* or *closed*). Choose the register, enter the float and open the session. **There is at most one open
session per register**, even if two people try to open it at the same instant; different registers can be open
together.

## Reports per register

In [Corrispettivi](/en/gestore/corrispettivi/) the **Register** filter shows only the payments recorded in that
register's sessions. Online payments, which do not go through a session, appear only in the **All registers**
total. The session history shows the register of each session too.

## Z closing as PDF

After closing (or during the day, for a check) press **Z closing PDF**:

- the panel prepares the Z summary of the session and opens the browser's print dialog: choose **Save as PDF** to
  get the file, or print it;
- from the menu next to it choose **A4 PDF** or **80 mm receipt** (thermal printers), or **Open in a new tab**.

The document has the establishment header (legal name, address, VAT number when present), the monochrome Arenile
logo, the register, who opened and closed it, float, takings by method, cash movements, expected and counted cash
with the difference. Totals are computed by the server, the same as on the Cash desk page; payments made in
[test mode](/en/gestore/modalita-prova/) are not included.

<Aside type="note" title="Why the browser's print">
Arenile renders the summary as a print-ready page instead of generating a PDF on the server: no extra dependency to
maintain, selectable and accessible text, the same look as the receipts. The result is a PDF identical to one from
a generator (ADR 0019).
</Aside>

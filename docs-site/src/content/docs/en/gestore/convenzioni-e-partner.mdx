**Where:** Operations → **Partner e convenzioni** (Partners and agreements); the terms of an agreement in
Configuration → **Pricing** → **Codes and agreements**. **Who:** owner and manager for partners, agreements and
reports; the cashier chooses the agreement on the booking.

An agreement is a deal of the beach establishment with a **partner** (the nearby hotel, an association, a company,
a public body) or with a **customer category** (residents, protected categories). Arenile applies it at the desk and
online, keeps the **partner account** and prepares the **agreements register** to present to the municipality for
the beach concession tender.

## Partners and customer categories

<Screenshot name="admin-partners" alt="Partners and agreements: list of the partners with type, town, agreements and balance" />

- **New partner**: type (accommodation, public body, company, association, other), contact person and details, VAT
  number or tax code. A partner used by agreements or bookings cannot be deleted: deactivate it.
- **Customer categories**: groups such as *Residents* or *Club members*, with the register category and, if needed,
  the requirement to check. Customers join a category from their own page.

## The agreement

In **Pricing → Codes and agreements** each agreement has, besides the discount:

- **Recipients**: a partner, a customer category or individual customers, and the **register category**;
- **Fixed prices per pack**: for example *residents' day pass 12 €*, instead of the pack price;
- **Scope and validity**: packs, extras and seasons it applies to;
- **Quota**: maximum spots per day under the agreement and **reserved** spots; unused reserved spots go back on sale
  a few days before (3 by default). No desk can exceed the limit, not even selling at the same time, and a cap
  changed after the first sales counts too. Two agreements with reserved spots on the same zones are guaranteed one
  at a time, not added up: use different zones for independent reserves. A spot blocked from the panel or a panel
  hold turned into a booking do not go through this check;
- **Requirement to check** (for example the hotel's confirmation or the membership card) and **Channels**: desk,
  online code, **agreement link** to give to the partner.

Every change creates a new version: bookings already made keep the terms they were sold with.

## At the desk

<Screenshot name="admin-booking-agreement" alt="New booking with the Residents agreement chosen, the requirement to check and the group data" />

<Steps>

1. In **New booking**, section **Agreement and guests**, choose the **Agreement**: if the customer has a default
   one it is already proposed. The price is recalculated at once.
2. If the agreement has a requirement, tick **Requirement verified**: only the date and the operator are recorded,
   never a copy of a document.
3. If needed, set the partner the guests come from, the **group name**, adults and children, the guest names and the
   share **charged to the partner**.

</Steps>

On the map, bookings with an agreement have the **Agreement or partner** glyph, and the quick filter shows only
those of one agreement. From the booking detail you can change the agreement after seeing the price difference.

## Online: code and partner link

With the online channel the agreement has a link such as `/b/<establishment>?c=BELVEDERE` to give to the partner
(for example on the hotel's website): whoever opens it sees the agreement box and the discount is already applied to
the quote. The customer can also type the code in the **What** step. An unknown, expired or desk-only code is never
revealed.

## Partner account

<Screenshot name="admin-partner-account" alt="Partner account with opening balance, charged, paid and closing balance" />

On the partner's page, for a season or a period: **opening balance**, **charged** (the partner's share of its
bookings), **paid** and **closing balance** (opening + charged − paid). **Record payment** registers the transfers
received; a wrong payment is **voided** with a reason, never deleted. Cancelled, expired or rejected bookings charge
nothing to the partner. **Export** produces the statement in Excel or as a page to print or save as PDF.

## Reports and register for the tender

<Screenshot name="admin-agreements-register" alt="Agreements register with agreements, bookings, spot-days, people and discount granted" />

**Rendiconti** (Reports, from the Partners and agreements page) has two views for a season or a period:

- **Agreements register**: for each agreement the terms in plain Italian, bookings, spot-days, people, revenue and
  **discount granted**, with totals per category (residents, accommodation, protected categories…) and the grand
  total. **Export** in Excel or as a printable page with room for date and signature, meant to show the
  municipality that the commitments of the tender offer were kept;
- **Report per partner**: bookings, days, people, total and partner share, in Excel or CSV.

<Aside type="note" title="Same numbers as the cash desk">
The register and the reports use the same amounts as the cash reports (test bookings are excluded): at the end of
the season the register's revenue matches the booked and collected revenue of the same bookings.
</Aside>

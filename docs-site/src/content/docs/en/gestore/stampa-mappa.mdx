<Screenshot name="admin-map-print" alt="Print menu of the day map" />

## Day map

<Steps>

1. Set the day, the slot, the **Labels** (Code, Customer, End, Setup) and, if needed, a quick filter on the map: the
   print shows what you see.
2. Press **Print** and choose **A4 landscape map** or **A3 landscape map**.
3. In the browser's print dialog choose the printer, the paper and landscape orientation, or **Save as PDF**.

</Steps>

The printed map has the legend of the statuses and glyphs: arrival, departure, agreement or partner, group, extras,
note.

## Lifeguard list

**Lifeguard list** prepares an A4 page with the occupied spots row by row: the surname of the occupant, the
setup (for example *2L 1S*), the day's arrivals and departures and who still has a balance to pay. It contains no
phone numbers or emails.

<Aside type="tip" title="Labels and filters">
The **Labels** combine (for example code and end date) and are remembered for your user. The **quick filters**
(Arrivals, Departures, Balance due, Present, one agreement, one group) highlight the matching spots and dim the
others.
</Aside>

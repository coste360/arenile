**Where:** Configuration → **Users**. **Who:** **owners** only.

Everyone who uses the panel has their own user, with a **role** that decides what they can do. Permissions are checked
in the core of the program, not just by hiding buttons: a role cannot do what it is not allowed to, not even by calling
the APIs.

<Screenshot name="admin-team" alt="The establishment's users with role, status and two-step verification" />

## Roles

| Role | For whom |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | The owner: everything, including users and payment credentials. |
| **Manager** | Whoever runs the establishment: configuration, prices, bookings, cash desk, refunds, reports. |
| **Cashier** | Whoever works the desk: bookings, payments and cash desk, check-in. |
| **Beach staff** | Whoever does the rounds: map, check-in, releases. |
| **Read only** | Whoever only needs to look: accountant, partner. Map, bookings, reports. |

## Adding a user

<Steps>

1. **Invite user**.
2. **Email**, **Name**, **Role**, panel **Language**.
3. **Scope**: **This establishment** or **Whole organization** (all the organization's establishments).
4. **Initial password**: optional. If you leave it empty one is generated and **shown only once**: share it securely.
5. **Add user**.

</Steps>

If the email already has an account (for example a colleague from another establishment), the user only gets the new
role and keeps their password.

## Managing users

- **Role of…**: change the role from the list.
- **Deactivate** / **Reactivate**: a deactivated user cannot sign in and their open sessions are closed at once.
  Always deactivate people who no longer work at the beach.
- **Revoke access**: removes access to this establishment.
- The **2FA** column shows who has two-step verification enabled.

<Aside type="note" title="Always at least one owner">
  Arenile prevents removing, deactivating or demoting the last active owner: the establishment is never left without
  someone who can manage users and payments.
</Aside>

## Two-step verification

With two-step verification (TOTP), signing in also requires the 6-digit code of an authenticator app. Whoever runs the
server can make it mandatory for owners with `ARENILE_REQUIRE_TOTP_FOR_OWNERS=true`: in that case an owner without 2FA
is asked to enable it before continuing.

## Good practice

- One user per person: never shared accounts, so the activity log says who did what.
- The minimum role needed: beach staff do not need the cash desk.
- The accountant as **Read only**: they see the daily takings without being able to change anything.

## Searching

**Search** by name, phone or email (no need to worry about accents) and filter by **Tag**. Anonymised customers are
hidden: **Show anonymised** to see them. The **Marketing** column says whether the customer gave consent.

## New customer

**New customer** has one required field: the **Last name**. **Phone** and **Email** are optional (used to contact
the customer and to spot duplicates, only when given). Then, also optional:

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **First name**, **Language** | The language of emails to the customer. |
| **Tags** | Comma separated, e.g. `vip, family, regular` (up to 20, 30 characters each). The `vip` tag shows the VIP indicator on the map; tags are also used in price rules (**Customer tags**). |
| **Internal notes** | Never shown to the customer. Do not write health data. |
| **Marketing consent** | Optional and separate: date, source and text version are recorded. |

Arenile avoids duplicates: if the email or phone already belongs to a customer, it suggests them (*Possibly already
registered* → **Use this one**).

## The customer record

<Screenshot name="admin-customer-detail" alt="Customer record with profile, statistics, bookings, season passes, credits and GDPR actions" />

- **Profile** with the data and the consent ("Consent given on… (source)").
- Statistics: **Bookings**, **Total spent**, **Balance due**, **Available credit**.
- **Recent bookings**, **Season passes** (with the released half-days), **Credits** with their movements (source:
  Cancellation, Season pass release, Bad weather, Manual, Redemption) and any expiry.
- **New booking** for this customer.

Customers booking online are created when they enter their details at the "Details and payment" step; the public
page never links them to an existing customer with the same email or phone (whoever knows a contact detail could
read their data): when the same person books again, the customer card flags the duplicate. Marketing consent given
online has source "online".

<Aside type="note" title="The «Walk-in customer»">
  Quick sales without a name (ADR 0020) are recorded on a single system customer, **Cliente al banco** (tag
  `walk-in`), created on first use: it will carry hundreds of bookings. It is not a person: it stays out of the top
  customers ranking and of the renewal campaigns, asks for no consent and is never anonymised or deleted. The optional
  name of a ticket stays on the ticket and never becomes a customer.
</Aside>

## GDPR rights

### Export

**Export data (GDPR)** downloads a JSON file with all the customer's data: a ready answer to an access or portability
request.

### Anonymisation

<Steps>

1. In the record press **Anonymise**.
2. Read the warning: name, contacts, notes and consents are replaced **for good**; bookings and payments stay for
   accounting, without personal data.
3. To confirm, type the customer's last name.
4. **Anonymise permanently**.

</Steps>

<Aside type="danger" title="This cannot be undone">
  There is no way back, not even for the server administrator. Export the data first if the customer requested it.
</Aside>

Cancellation reasons and notes on payments and credits are deleted too. For the full picture (roles, privacy notice,
response times) see [Privacy and GDPR](/en/installazione/privacy/).

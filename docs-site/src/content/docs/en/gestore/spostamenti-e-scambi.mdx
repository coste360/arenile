**Where:** Operations → **Map** (spot sheet → **Move**) and booking detail → **More actions**. **Who:** whoever may
move bookings (owner, manager, cashier).

## Moving from a date

<Steps>

1. On the day map tap the spot and press **Move**.
2. Tap the free target spot.
3. In **From when** choose **Whole stay**, **From today**, **From tomorrow** or **From a date**.
4. Check the price difference and press **Move**.

</Steps>

<Screenshot name="admin-map-move-from" alt="Move from D3 to F1 with the From when choice" />

With a later date the booking stays on the first spot until the day before and moves to the new one from that
date: "from 15 August it moves to A7". Past days do not change. If the two spots have different price categories,
each day is priced with the spot occupied that day.

## Swapping two bookings

If in **Move** mode you tap an **occupied** spot, the panel proposes a **swap**: the two bookings exchange their
spots on the days they share, from the day shown onwards; on the other days each stays where it was.

<Screenshot name="admin-map-swap" alt="Preview of the swap between D3 and D6 with the price difference of both" />

The preview shows the period of the swap and the **price difference of each booking**. Press **Swap** to confirm.
The swap is one single operation: at no moment is a spot taken twice, and if one of the two bookings was changed
meanwhile nothing changes.

## Moving a group

For bookings with the same **group name** the spot sheet has **Move the group**: tap the new position of the chosen
spot and the others follow it keeping the arrangement (the distances between the spots). You see the new spots
before confirming; the move is all or nothing.

## Undoing the last move

After a move, a swap or a group move, a notice with **Undo** stays for **5 minutes**, and the map shows **Undo last
move**. You can undo only your own move, only if the bookings did not change afterwards and the original spots are
still free.

## Duplicate and other actions

In the booking detail, **More actions** contains:

- **Duplicate**: same spot, customer, extras, agreement and group data on new dates, re-priced, with a warning if the
  spots are not free;
- **Change customer**, with a preview of the price difference;
- **Summary via WhatsApp**: the text ready in the customer's language (dates, spots, amount, balance and a new manage
  link), with **Copy text** and **Open WhatsApp** on the customer's number; only when the customer has a phone.
  Arenile sends nothing: you confirm the message from your own app;
- **Print summary**: a summary for the customer with the booking QR code, on A4 or an 80 mm receipt.

<Aside type="note" title="History">
Every move, swap and undo appears in the booking history with the operator and the time.
</Aside>

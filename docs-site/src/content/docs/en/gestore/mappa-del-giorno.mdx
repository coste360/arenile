**Where:** Operations → **Map**. **Who:** every role views; check-ins and releases also beach staff; bookings, moves
and payments cashiers, managers and owners.

The operational map is the beach of one day, spot by spot, with the status of each. It is shared in real time
between the desk, the beach and online bookings.

<Screenshot name="admin-map" alt="Operational map of the day with coloured spot statuses and the legend" />

## Choosing day and slot

The bar at the top is always visible: **Previous day**, **Next day**, **Today**, the **Date** picker, the **Slot**
(**Full day**, **Morning**, **Afternoon**) and, if you have more than one, the **Map**. The **Map** / **List** switch
shows the same spots as a list by row, handy on a phone or with a screen reader.

On closure days or off season **Establishment closed** appears.

## Statuses and legend

Every spot has a **coloured ring** around the drawing and, for booked statuses, a **symbol** in the corner: the map can
be read without telling colours apart, in light and dark theme. The legend is always above the map.

<StatusBadges />

| Status | Meaning | Symbol |
|---|---|---|
| **Free** | Not occupied | none (faint halo) |
| **Booked online** | Booking confirmed from the public page | globe |
| **Booked at the desk** | Booking confirmed from the panel | desk |
| **Season pass** | Spot of a season pass holder | star |
| **Season pass holder away today** | The holder released the day: the spot is on sale | outgoing arrow |
| **Pending** | Online checkout or approval in progress: spot held | clock |
| **Balance due** | Booking with an open balance | euro |
| **Checked in** | Check-in recorded | tick |
| **Blocked** | Manual block (with reason) | cross |
| **Renewal reserved** | Reserved for the season pass renewal | renewal |
| **Unavailable** | Spot inactive or closed | — |

Additional indicators show **Extras**, **Note** and **VIP** customers (customer tag).

## Searching

The **Search customer, code or spot** field highlights the matching spots on the map ("3 spots found"): useful when a
customer at the desk only gives their surname.

## The spot sheet

<Screenshot name="admin-map-spot" alt="Operational map with the sheet of an occupied spot open" />

Tap a spot to open its sheet: **Booking** or **Season pass**, **Code**, **Period**, **Slot**, **Packs**, **Status**,
**Total**, **Paid** and **Balance**. If it is pending, it shows how long the hold lasts; if it is blocked, the reason.
Actions depend on the status and your role:

| Action | When |
|---|---|
| **Check in** | Today's booking not yet checked in |
| **New booking** | Free spot: opens the booking sheet already filled in |
| **Release day** | Absent pass holder: the spot becomes bookable; if it is resold, the holder gets the credit set in the policy |
| **Revoke release** | Cancels a release not yet resold |
| **Move** | Moves the booking to another spot |
| **Collect** | Records a payment on the balance |
| **Open details** | Opens the full [booking](/en/gestore/prenotazioni/) |

### Moving a booking

<Steps>

1. In the sheet press **Move**: the map asks for the destination spot.
2. Tap a free spot.
3. Read the preview: Arenile checks availability and price (*The price does not change*, *Difference to collect*,
   *Difference to refund*) and lists any conflicts day by day.
4. **Move**. The move applies from the day shown for the rest of the stay.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note" title="No double selling, ever">
  If an online customer takes the same spot at the same instant, only one of the two operations succeeds and the
  other gets a clear message with the conflicting dates. The guarantee comes from the database, not from who clicks
  faster.
</Aside>

## On the phone

<Screenshot name="admin-map-mobile" alt="Operational map on a smartphone, used by beach staff" />

Beach staff can do their round with a phone: map with pinch-zoom and pan, **Fit to view**, spot sheet, one-tap
check-in. When labels get too small, a notice suggests zooming in or switching to the list.

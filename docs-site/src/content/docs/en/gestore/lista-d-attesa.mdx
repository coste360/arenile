**Where:** Operations → **Waitlist**. **Who:** cashiers, managers and owners; the settings whoever can configure
the establishment.

When there is no room for the requested dates, the customer joins the **waitlist**. As soon as a compatible spot
frees up (cancellation, day released by a pass holder, expired booking), Arenile holds it for the first in line and
emails them the link to book it.

<Screenshot name="admin-waitlist" alt="Waitlist: queue with position, period, request, status and running offer with the time left" />

## Settings

**Settings** opens the options valid for the whole establishment:

- **Waitlist active**: off, no entries are accepted and no offers are sent.
- **Join from the public page**: for now through the API only (`POST /api/public/v1/{slug}/waitlist`, for
  websites and integrations); the booking page has no join form yet, so the switch is not shown in the panel.
- **Automatic offers**: when a spot frees up an offer goes to the first in line; off, offers are only sent by hand.
- **Default offer duration (minutes)**: how long the spot stays held waiting for the answer.

## Adding a customer

<Steps>

1. **New entry**.
2. Choose the **Customer**, the **Period**, the **Time slot** and the **Pack**.
3. Optional: **Zone** (or **Any zone**), **Quantity** and **Note**.
4. **Add to waitlist**.

</Steps>

Customers who join through the public API (a website or an integration calling
`POST /api/public/v1/{slug}/waitlist`) get an email with the **personal link** to their request, where they can
check it or leave the list. Every new email renews the link: only the latest one works. One open request per
address, pack and period: repeats create nothing and send no further email.

## The queue

The order is the **#** column: the higher, the sooner the offer. The **Move up** and **Move down** arrows change the
priority; an entry with a running offer has no number until it is answered.

From the row menu:

- **Offer a spot**: choose the **Spot** (or **The first matching one**) and the **Offer duration (minutes)**, then
  **Send the offer**.
- **Revoke the offer**: the spot is free again and goes to the next one.
- **Edit the request** or **Remove from the waitlist**.

Clicking the name opens the detail with the history of the **Offers** (when, which spot, why, outcome).

## What the customer sees

The offer email leads to the request page: **Spot … has freed up for you!** with the deadline.

- **Book now** continues in the normal online booking, already at the details and payment step. The confirmation
  comes as always only from the payment.
- **Decline** frees the spot, which goes to the next in line; the customer stays on the list (or leaves it, if
  they choose so).

<Aside type="note" title="One spot, one offer">
The hold on the spot is guaranteed by the database: even when many spots free up together, each freed spot
produces at most one offer. An expired, declined or revoked offer goes to the next one, never back to the same
person for that spot. Entries from the panel follow the desk rules; entries from the website follow the online
rules and payment.
</Aside>

When a customer asks for their data to be erased (GDPR), their entries are removed.

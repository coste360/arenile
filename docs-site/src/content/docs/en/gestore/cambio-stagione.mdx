**Where:** Configuration → **Seasons and periods** → **New season from this one**. **Who:** whoever can configure
the establishment (managers and owners).

At the end of the season Arenile prepares the next one by copying the configuration of the season just closed,
with the dates moved to the new year. Catalogue, maps and spots do not depend on the season and stay as they are.
Right after, you can [propose the renewals](/en/gestore/rinnovi/) to the pass holders: the whole cycle happens in
the panel, with no database work.

## Creating the new season

<Steps>

1. **1. Starting season and dates**: choose the **Source season**, the **Year of the new season** and check
   **Opening** and **Closing** (proposed by moving the dates by one year).
2. **2. What to copy**: keep on the blocks to copy: **Rate periods**, **Closure days**, **Price rules**, **Fixed
   prices**, **Policies**.
3. **Calculate preview**: the **3. Preview of season** box shows how many periods, closures, rules, fixed prices and
   policies will be created and how many **Pass holders to renew** there are. Nothing is written yet.
4. Check the **To check** warnings (for example a closure that could not be copied), then **Create season** and
   confirm.

</Steps>

The new season starts as a **draft**: check dates and prices in [Seasons and periods](/en/gestore/stagioni-e-periodi/)
and in the [price list](/en/gestore/prezzi/) before activating it.

### How dates are moved

- Dates move by one year keeping the weekday where it makes sense (an event on a Saturday stays on a Saturday),
  while month starts and ends and fixed holidays such as 15 August keep their calendar date.
- A closure that would fall outside the new season, or on a day already closed, is not copied and shows up among
  the warnings.
- The preview and the creation run exactly the same computation: the preview numbers are what you get.

<Aside type="tip" title="Repeating creates no duplicates">
If you press **Create season** again for the same year, Arenile shows the season already created by this rollover
instead of creating another one. If a season of that year already exists and was created by hand, the wizard
stops with a warning: fix the existing season first.
</Aside>

## After the creation

From the **Season created** box you can:

- **Open the season** to review dates and periods;
- **Offer renewals to N pass holders**: opens the [Renewals](/en/gestore/rinnovi/) page with the new campaign
  already set on the two seasons.

At the bottom of the page, **Rollovers already run** lists the rollovers done, with what was copied and when.

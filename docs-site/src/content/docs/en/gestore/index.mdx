Demo** and tips from the field. Screenshots are regenerated at every release, so they always match the program you
are using.

## How the panel is organised

- **Sidebar**: at the top the **Establishment** switcher (with **+ New establishment**), then the **Operations**
  group and the **Configuration** group. On a phone it opens with the menu button.
- **Command palette**: press <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> (<kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>K</kbd> on Mac) to search for a page
  and jump to it without the mouse.
- **User menu** at the bottom: **Light**, **Dark** or **System** theme, and **Sign out**.
- **Real time**: map, today, planning and cash desk refresh by themselves when a colleague or an online customer
  books, pays or checks in. No need to reload.
- **Languages**: the panel is available in Italian, English, German and French.

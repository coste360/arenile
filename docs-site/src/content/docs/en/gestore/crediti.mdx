**Where:** Operations → **Customers** → customer page → **Credit movements**. **Who:** granting credit and
adjustments needs the permission to issue refunds; using it only needs to be able to record payments.

**Credit** is an amount the customer can spend at the establishment as a payment method. It accrues automatically
when the [policy](/en/gestore/policy/) says so (cancellation, bad weather, released and resold pass days) or you
grant it by hand.

<Screenshot name="admin-customer-credits" alt="Customer page with available credit, expiring credit and ledger of movements" />

## Balance and movements

- **Available credit**: what the customer can spend today.
- **Expiring**: the credit expiring soon, with the date.
- The table lists every movement: **Cancellation**, **Pass release**, **Bad weather**, **Manual credit**,
  **Goodwill**, **Use**, **Expiry**, **Adjustment**, with the linked booking.

The balance is always the sum of the movements and **can never go negative**, not even when two cashiers use it at
the same moment. Expired credit is removed automatically.

## Granting credit

<Steps>

1. **Grant credit**.
2. Choose the **Type**: **Goodwill**, **Manual credit** or **Adjustment** (in that case **Add** or **Remove**).
3. Enter the **Amount**, optionally the **Expiry** (empty = validity set by the policy) and a **Note**.
4. **Grant** (or **Remove from balance**).

</Steps>

## Using the credit

- From the customer page: **Use on a booking**, choose the booking with a balance due and the amount (at most the
  smaller of credit and balance), then **Use the credit**.
- From a booking: **Record payment** with the **Customer credit** method; the panel shows the available credit.
- Customers can use it themselves from the booking's manage link (**Use … of credit for the balance**) and when
  accepting a [renewal](/en/gestore/rinnovi/).

<Aside type="note" title="Credit goes back as credit">
When you refund a booking paid partly with credit, that part goes back to the customer's credit balance, never as
money. The part paid with other methods follows the refund policy.
</Aside>

Credit is included in the customer's data export (GDPR); with anonymisation the amounts stay for the accounts and
the notes disappear.

**Where:** Operations → **Season passes**. **Who:** cashiers, managers and owners; releases also beach staff.

A **season pass** reserves a spot to a customer for the whole season. When the holder is not coming, they can
**release** the day: the spot goes back on sale and, if it is resold, the holder accrues a credit.

<Screenshot name="admin-passes" alt="The season's passes grouped by zone and row, with releases, credit and balance" />

## List

The season's passes are grouped by zone and row, with **Spot**, **Subscriber**, **Period**, **Status**, **Releases**,
**Credit** and **Balance**.

## New season pass

<Steps>

1. **New season pass**.
2. Choose the **Subscriber**, the **Season**, the **Pack** and the **Time slot**.
3. Choose the **Spot** among those free **for the whole season**.
4. Optional: **From** / **To** (empty = season opening and closing) and **Internal notes**.
5. Check the **List price** and the deposit due, then **Create season pass**.

</Steps>

The price comes from the price list with the **Season** formula (usually a
[fixed-duration price](/en/gestore/prezzi/#fixed-duration-prices)). For an agreed price use an agreement or a
dedicated price rule. Payments and cancellation are handled from the linked booking (**Open the booking**).

## Releasing days

<Steps>

1. Open the pass: the release calendar shows the days **Released**, **Resold** and those you are **Selecting**.
2. Tap the days to release and choose the **Part of the day to release**.
3. Optional: a **Note**.
4. **Release N days**: the spot is immediately bookable at the desk and online.

</Steps>

A single day can also be released from the [map of the day](/en/gestore/mappa-del-giorno/) with **Release day**; the
spot appears as **Season pass holder away today**.

If the [policy](/en/gestore/policy/) allows it (**Customer can release season days**), the holder releases days by
themselves from the manage area with the magic link.

### Revoking a release

**Revoke** (in the **Released days** list) gives the spot back to the holder. It is not possible if the day has
already been resold.

### Credit for resold days

With **Credit for released days** enabled in the policy, every released **and resold** day earns the holder a credit
equal to the chosen percentage: you see it in **Credit accrued** and on the customer record. The credit never goes
below zero.

## Manual price

The price of a season pass comes from the price list. For a special agreement (long-standing customer, agreed
package) the owner or the manager can replace it:

<Screenshot name="admin-pass-price" alt="Season pass sheet with the Price section" />

<Steps>

1. Open the pass: the **Price** section shows the **Total**.
2. **Set manual price**, enter the **New total** and the **Reason** (required).
3. **Save price**.

</Steps>

The section then shows **Manual price**, the **Price list amount** computed by the engine, the reason and when it
was changed. **Back to price list** recomputes the price from the price list. The new total cannot be lower than
what was already paid. Every change goes to the activity log with previous price, new price and reason; the price
breakdown accepted at creation stays unchanged. The cashier role sees the price but cannot change it.

## New season and renewals

At the end of the season the [season rollover](/en/gestore/cambio-stagione/) prepares the next season and a
[renewal](/en/gestore/rinnovi/) campaign offers every pass holder the same spot, with right of pre-emption. The
credit accrued by the holder is used as a payment method at the desk, from the booking's manage link and when
accepting the renewal (see [Customer credits](/en/gestore/crediti/)).

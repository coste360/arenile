**Where:** Operations → **Quick sale** (also from the **Quick sale** button of the **Cash desk**) or from the
**Map**: tap a free spot and choose **Sell day ticket**. **Who:** cashiers, managers and owners.

The daily sale is built for walk-in customers: one screen, a few taps, no mandatory personal data. Every sale creates
a **confirmed** desk booking (the same as panel bookings: history, payments and reports see it) and a **daily
numbered ticket** with a QR code.

<Screenshot name="admin-daily-sale" alt="Quick sale: where, formula, pack, extras, total with VAT and payment" />

## Selling a day

<Steps>

1. **Where**: pick a precise **Spot** or **Zone only** (for example *Front row*). Each zone shows how many free
   places it has for the chosen slot.
2. **Formula**: **Full day**, **Morning** or **Afternoon**. The day is today; from the map of another day the sale is
   for that day.
3. **Pack** and **Extras** (with the + and − buttons). The **Total** comes from the day's price list, with VAT shown.
4. **Name on the ticket** (optional): only last and first name for the map label. No phone, no email.
5. Take the payment in **Cash** (the [cash desk](/en/gestore/cassa/) must be open; enter the **Cash received** to see
   the **Change**) or by **Card**.

</Steps>

The ticket is issued at once: the **Ticket issued** box shows the big number (**#037**), with **Reprint** and **New
sale**. When automatic printing is on, the print starts too.

<Screenshot name="admin-daily-sale-map" alt="Day map with the Sell day panel open on a free spot" />

On the desk computer, shortcuts speed things up: **1**, **2**, **3** for the slot, **P** spot, **Z** zone, **C** cash,
**K** card. On a phone, total and payment stay pinned at the bottom of the screen.

<Aside type="caution" title="No sales offline">
  Only the server guarantees availability (database constraints): offline the quick sale is disabled so the same
  place is never sold twice.
</Aside>

## The ticket number

- It is a **progressive number per day of validity** (establishment time zone), shared by umbrellas and entries: the
  first ticket valid today is **#001** and the sequence is unique for the establishment, across registers and
  devices. Selling for another day uses that day's numbering (tomorrow starts again at #001 while today goes on).
- A **voided** ticket keeps its number, marked *Voided*: the only case where the sequence has a visible gap.
- The QR code holds a **random code** of the ticket, not the booking code (which is sequential and stays printed
  under the QR): staff check it with their phone and nobody can guess it.

## Printing

Tickets print from the browser, no software to install: **80 mm receipt**, **58 mm receipt**, **A5** or **A4**. They
show the establishment name and logo, the number and, large under it, the spot or zone (*to assign*), the date
(*Valid on …*), formula, pack, extras, total with the VAT breakdown, payment method, the line **Not valid for tax
purposes** (the fiscal document is the payment's, from the cash desk) and the QR code.

<Screenshot name="admin-ticket-print" alt="Print preview of the ticket in the 80 mm receipt format" />

**Reprint** produces the same copy marked *Reprint* and is recorded in the history.

In **Today's tickets** → **Settings** (managers and owners) choose the **Default print format**, **Print right after
the sale** and **Sale by zone**; they apply to every register.

## Today's tickets

**Where:** Operations → **Today's tickets**. The tickets issued on a day with **Number**, **Time**, **Spot or zone**,
**Formula**, **Name**, **Status** (*Assigned*, *To assign*, *Issued*, *Entered*, *Voided*), **Amount** and
**Method**.

<Screenshot name="admin-tickets" alt="Today's tickets: summary, ticket list and takings by VAT rate" />

- The **Day summary** counts issued tickets, takings by method, tickets to assign and voided ones.
- **Takings by VAT rate**: the same amounts as the cash session and the [daily takings](/en/gestore/corrispettivi/);
  voided tickets are left out.
- **Export CSV** downloads the day's list (`;` separator, euro amounts with a decimal comma).
- For each ticket: **Print**/**Reprint**, **Assign** or **Move** (see [Spot assignment](/en/gestore/assegnazione-ombrelloni/))
  and **Void ticket**.

## Voiding a ticket

<Steps>

1. **Void ticket** on the ticket's row.
2. Write the **Reason** (required, kept in the history).
3. Choose the **Refund**: **Refund with the same method** (cash out of the open register or a recorded card
   reversal), **Credit to the customer** (only for a ticket of a customer on file) or **No refund**.

</Steps>

The spot or the place in the zone becomes free again; the ticket keeps its number and is marked as voided.

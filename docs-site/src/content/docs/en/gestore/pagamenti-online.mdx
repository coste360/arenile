**Where:** Configuration → **Online payments**. **Who:** **owners** only (payment credentials are reserved to the
owner).

With Stripe customers pay online by card and wallet (Apple Pay, Google Pay and the other methods you enable on
Stripe) and the money goes **straight to the establishment's Stripe account**. Arenile takes nothing: you pay only
Stripe's fees.

<Screenshot name="admin-payment-settings" alt="Online payments: Stripe provider, mode, keys and webhook URL" />

## How it works (in short)

<Steps>

1. The customer completes the steps and presses **Pay**: the spot stays held for them while they pay (up to 30
   minutes).
2. **Stripe Checkout** opens at full window, even if the page was embedded in your website.
3. Stripe collects the money and notifies Arenile with a **signed webhook**.
4. Arenile verifies the signature, records the payment and **confirms the booking**, all in a single operation. The
   customer returns to the page, which shows the confirmation, and receives the email.

</Steps>

<Aside type="caution" title="Confirmation only comes from Stripe">
  A booking is never confirmed because the customer's browser "comes back" from the payment page: only Stripe's
  signed webhook (or reconciliation, which queries Stripe) can confirm it. That is why **the webhook must be
  configured**: without it payments reach Stripe but bookings stay pending until reconciliation recovers them.
</Aside>

## Step-by-step setup

<Steps>

1. **Create or open the establishment's Stripe account** at [stripe.com](https://stripe.com) and complete the
   business details. You can start in **test mode** before verification is complete.

2. **Copy the API keys.** In the Stripe dashboard open **Developers → API keys** (with the "Test mode" switch on
   for test keys):
   - the **publishable key** (`pk_test_…` or `pk_live_…`);
   - the **secret key** (`sk_test_…` or `sk_live_…`).

3. **In Arenile** open **Configuration → Online payments**, choose the **Stripe Checkout** provider, **Test mode**
   and paste the two keys. Save.

4. **Copy the webhook URL** shown on the page. It looks like this:

   ```text
   https://prenota.mio-lido.it/webhooks/payments/stripe/<establishment-id>
   ```

5. **Create the webhook on Stripe.** In **Developers → Webhooks → Add endpoint**: paste the URL and select these
   events:

   | Event | Purpose |
   |---|---|
   | `checkout.session.completed` | Payment completed with Checkout: confirms the booking |
   | `payment_intent.succeeded` | Payment succeeded (also with the embedded form) |
   | `payment_intent.payment_failed` | Payment failed: the customer can retry while the hold is valid |
   | `charge.refunded` | Refund made on Stripe: recorded in Arenile |

6. **Copy the webhook signing secret** (`whsec_…`, "Signing secret") and paste it into Arenile as the webhook
   secret. Save.

7. **Verify.** Press **Check credentials**: Arenile asks Stripe for the account details and shows the
   business name if the keys are right.

8. **Try a booking** from the public page, ideally from your phone, with the test card `4242 4242 4242 4242` (any
   future date and CVC). Check that the confirmation arrives and that the booking is **Confirmed** and paid in the
   panel.

9. **Go live.** Repeat steps 2–6 with the "Test mode" switch off (`live` keys and a new live webhook with its own
   secret) and choose **Live payments** in Arenile.

</Steps>

<Aside type="note" title="Your keys are safe">
  Secret keys are encrypted before being saved and **are never shown again**: the panel only says they are configured
  and shows the last four characters. To change them paste the new value; leaving the field untouched keeps the saved
  key. Test and live credentials are saved separately. They never end up in logs or in the activity log.
</Aside>

How much is paid online (everything, a deposit or nothing) is decided by the [policy](/en/gestore/policy/) scoped to
the Online channel. When the online policy has payment on arrival, the online booking is confirmed without payment and paid at the desk.

## Manual payment

If you choose the **No online payment** provider (or do not configure Stripe) there is no online payment: online bookings are
confirmed (or go to approval) and the balance is collected at the [cash desk](/en/gestore/cassa/) in cash, card or
bank transfer.

## Reconciliation

The internet is not perfect: a webhook can get lost. That is why Arenile, **every 15 minutes**, asks Stripe for the
outcome of payments pending for more than 10 minutes and applies the result with the same procedure as the webhook. A
webhook arriving late is never applied twice.

The **reconciliation report** compares the payments at Stripe with Arenile's ledger and flags:

| Issue | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Pending for more than 10 minutes | The payment has no outcome yet: usually a missing webhook |
| Different outcome | Stripe says "succeeded" or "failed" and Arenile does not |
| Payment without ledger entry | Stripe collected but the ledger has no matching row |
| Different amount | The amounts do not match |
| Provider unreachable | Stripe did not answer or the driver cannot check |

You can also run reconciliation by hand from the same report.

## Refunds

From the [booking detail](/en/gestore/prenotazioni/#refunds), a refund on a payment made with Stripe can be made
**through Stripe**: the money goes back to the customer's card. The refund stays **Pending** until Stripe confirms it
(`charge.refunded` webhook or reconciliation), then becomes **Succeeded**. A refund made directly from the Stripe
dashboard is also recorded in Arenile. A refund can never exceed what was paid.

Cancelling a booking computes the refund from the terms the customer accepted; you can refund in money or as credit
to the customer.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | What to check |
|---|---|
| The credentials check fails | Secret key of the wrong mode (test or live) or revoked key |
| Payment succeeded on Stripe but booking still pending | Webhook not created, wrong URL (domain, establishment id) or wrong signing secret. The Stripe dashboard shows delivery errors; reconciliation recovers within 15 minutes |
| Stripe reports `400` errors on the webhook | Wrong signing secret or one from the other mode (test vs live) |
| The customer sees "The payment service is not responding" | Stripe temporarily unreachable: the page invites them to retry shortly |
| The hold expired during payment | The customer took more than 30 minutes: if the payment arrives anyway, check the reconciliation report and handle the booking from the panel |

**Where:** Configuration → **Email and notifications**. **Who:** owners and managers.

Arenile sends transactional emails from **your** mail server (SMTP): no imposed service, no extra cost. Emails go out
automatically from booking events, queued and retried, in the customer's language.

<Screenshot name="admin-email-settings" alt="Email and notifications: sender, customer and manager notifications, delivery log" />

## The mail server

The transport is configured on the server by whoever installed Arenile and is shown read-only:

| Transport | When | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **SMTP** | `ARENILE_SMTP_URL` set | Emails are actually sent |
| **File** | no SMTP, `ARENILE_MAIL_DIR` set | Each email is saved as an `.eml` file (development and trials) |
| **Log** | neither | Emails are not sent: the log keeps only the template and the masked recipient |

The sender is `ARENILE_MAIL_FROM` (see [Configuration](/en/installazione/configurazione/#email)). For good
deliverability set up **SPF, DKIM and DMARC** on the sender's domain.

## Notifications

<Tabs>
  <TabItem label="To the customer">
    | Notification | When |
    |---|---|
    | **Confirmation** | Booking confirmed (after payment, approval or desk confirmation). Contains code, summary, inline **QR code**, **`.ics`** calendar file and the **manage link** |
    | **Balance reminder** | Automatic, before the balance due date: set up in **Customer emails** (see below) |
    | **Day-before reminder** | Automatic, the day before arrival: set up in **Customer emails** |
    | **Cancellation** | Booking cancelled or rejected |
    | **Refund** | Refund made |
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="To the manager">
    | Notification | When |
    |---|---|
    | **New online booking** | Every booking confirmed from the public page |
    | **Approval request** | A booking awaits your approval (for example a gazebo) |
    | **Payment failed** | An online payment did not go through |
    | **Cash closing with a difference** | *Not available yet* (planned by the specification) |
    | **Webhook delivery error** | *Not available yet* (planned by the specification) |
    | **Delivery error** | An email was not delivered after all retries |
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

Each notification can be switched on or off individually.

## Test email

Send a test email to an address of your choice to check server, sender and spam filters. The email is queued and
appears in the log straight away with its outcome.

## Delivery log

The log shows each message with template, linked booking, status (**queued**, **sending**, **sent**, **failed**,
**cancelled**) and number of attempts. Recipients are **masked** and email contents are not stored.

An email that fails is retried with growing back-off, up to 8 times; then it is marked failed and, if enabled, the
"Delivery error" notification goes to the manager. The same email is never sent twice for the same event.

## The manage link (magic link)

Every confirmation email contains a **personal link** the customer uses to open the **Your booking** area: details
and QR, balance payment, changes or cancellation within the rules, and for pass holders, day releases (see
[The customer journey](/en/gestore/percorso-del-cliente/)).

- The link is a 256-bit random code, stored only as a fingerprint: whoever has the database cannot rebuild it.
- It is valid until the end of the booking plus 30 days.
- If the customer loses it, from the public page they can **Request a new link** with email and booking code.

From the [booking detail](/en/gestore/prenotazioni/) staff can:

- **resend the confirmation email** (with a new manage link);
- **regenerate the link** (revoking the previous ones); the new link is shown **only once** so it can be shared from
  the desk, and can be emailed to the customer.

## Customer emails: texts and reminders

**Where:** Configuration → **Customer emails**. **Who:** owners and managers (other roles see the page read-only).

### Automatic reminders

| Reminder | When it is sent | Default |
|---|---|---|
| **Day before** | The day before the booking starts, at the chosen time | at 18:00 |
| **Balance** | Before the balance due date set by the [policy](/en/gestore/policy/) ("N days before" or on arrival), with the chosen lead time (0 to 30 days) | 2 days before, at 10:00 |

Times are in the establishment timezone, daylight saving time included. Each reminder is sent **only once** per
booking, even if the server restarts or runs on several instances. It is not sent for test, cancelled or expired
bookings, for customers without email and, for the balance, when nothing is left to pay (if the customer pays
after the reminder was queued, the sending is cancelled). A booking made after the sending time does not get that
day's reminder: it has just received its confirmation.

<Aside type="caution">
  If the server stays off for the whole day of a reminder, that reminder is not sent late the next day: better no
  reminder than one arriving after the stay has started.
</Aside>

### Email texts

For every customer email (confirmation, change, cancellation, request not accepted, request received, refund,
manage link, balance and day-before reminders) and every language you can change the **subject**, **opening
text**, **closing text** and **signature**.

<Steps>

1. Choose the **email** and the **language**.
2. Edit the texts. **Insert placeholder** adds booking data into the selected field.
3. Check the **preview**, updated while you type with a sample booking.
4. **Send a test to me** to see it in your inbox, then **Save**.

</Steps>

| Placeholder | Becomes |
|---|---|
| `{nome}`, `{cognome}` | Customer first and last name |
| `{codice}` | Booking code |
| `{data}` | Dates of the stay |
| `{postazione}` | Assigned spots |
| `{stabilimento}` | Establishment name |
| `{importo}` | Booking total (in the refund email: the refunded amount) |
| `{saldo}` | Balance due |
| `{scadenza}` | Balance due date (a date, or "on arrival") |

Placeholders keep their Italian names in every language. Texts are **plain text**: an empty line separates
paragraphs, no bold, links or HTML. A placeholder that is not in the list is flagged and cannot be saved. Limits:
subject 200 characters on one line, opening and closing texts 2000, signature 500.

These stay **fixed** and cannot be removed: QR code, summary with the amounts, balance notice, manage button and
link, cancellation terms, calendar note and footer. Logo and colours come from the theme of the
[public page](/en/gestore/pagina-pubblica/).

**Restore default** goes back to the Arenile text for that email and language. Until you customise a text, you
automatically get the improvements of the default texts with updates. Every save and restore is recorded in the
audit log.

## Frequently asked questions

**Emails end up in spam.**
Set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC for the `ARENILE_MAIL_FROM` domain and use a sender on your own domain, not a free
address.

**The customer says they did not receive the confirmation.**
Look up the booking in the delivery log: if it is **sent**, check spam and the address with them; if it is **failed**,
fix the customer's email and use **resend the confirmation** from the booking detail.

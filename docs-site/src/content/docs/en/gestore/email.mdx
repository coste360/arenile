**Where:** Configuration → **Email and notifications**. **Who:** owners and managers.

Arenile sends transactional emails from **your** mail server (SMTP): no imposed service, no extra cost. Emails go out
automatically from booking events, queued and retried, in the customer's language.

<Screenshot name="admin-email-settings" alt="Email and notifications: sender, customer and manager notifications, delivery log" />

## The mail server

The transport is configured on the server by whoever installed Arenile and is shown read-only:

| Transport | When | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **SMTP** | `ARENILE_SMTP_URL` set | Emails are actually sent |
| **File** | no SMTP, `ARENILE_MAIL_DIR` set | Each email is saved as an `.eml` file (development and trials) |
| **Log** | neither | Emails are not sent: the log keeps only the template and the masked recipient |

The sender is `ARENILE_MAIL_FROM` (see [Configuration](/en/installazione/configurazione/#email)). For good
deliverability set up **SPF, DKIM and DMARC** on the sender's domain.

## Notifications

  <TabItem label="To the customer">

    | **Balance reminder** | *Coming soon*: the switch is visible but disabled, the email is not sent yet |

  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="To the manager">
    | Notification | When |
    |---|---|
    | **New online booking** | Every booking confirmed from the public page |
    | **Approval request** | A booking awaits your approval (for example a gazebo) |
    | **Payment failed** | An online payment did not go through |
    | **Cash closing with a difference** | *Not available yet* (planned by the specification) |

    | **Delivery error** | An email was not delivered after all retries |
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

Each notification can be switched on or off individually.

## Test email

Send a test email to an address of your choice to check server, sender and spam filters. The email is queued and
appears in the log straight away with its outcome.

## Delivery log

The log shows each message with template, linked booking, status (**queued**, **sending**, **sent**, **failed**,
**cancelled**) and number of attempts. Recipients are **masked** and email contents are not stored.

An email that fails is retried with growing back-off, up to 8 times; then it is marked failed and, if enabled, the
"Delivery error" notification goes to the manager. The same email is never sent twice for the same event.

## The manage link (magic link)

Every confirmation email contains a **personal link** the customer uses to open the **Your booking** area: details
and QR, balance payment, changes or cancellation within the rules, and for pass holders, day releases (see
[The customer journey](/en/gestore/percorso-del-cliente/)).

- The link is a 256-bit random code, stored only as a fingerprint: whoever has the database cannot rebuild it.
- It is valid until the end of the booking plus 30 days.
- If the customer loses it, from the public page they can **Request a new link** with email and booking code.

From the [booking detail](/en/gestore/prenotazioni/) staff can:

- **resend the confirmation email** (with a new manage link);
- **regenerate the link** (revoking the previous ones); the new link is shown **only once** so it can be shared from
  the desk, and can be emailed to the customer.

## Templates

Email texts are Markdown templates, ready in Italian, English, German and French, with variables such as
`{{booking.code}}`, `{{customer.first_name}}` and `{{manage_url}}`. The email is composed at send time with the
booking's current data, as HTML and plain text, with the public page's colours and logo.

<Aside type="note">
  Editing templates from the panel, per establishment, is planned but not available yet: the default templates are
  used for now.
</Aside>

## Frequently asked questions

**Emails end up in spam.**
Set up SPF, DKIM and DMARC for the `ARENILE_MAIL_FROM` domain and use a sender on your own domain, not a free
address.

**The customer says they did not receive the confirmation.**
Look up the booking in the delivery log: if it is **sent**, check spam and the address with them; if it is **failed**,
fix the customer's email and use **resend the confirmation** from the booking detail.

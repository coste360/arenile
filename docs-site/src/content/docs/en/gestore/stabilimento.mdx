**Where:** Configuration → **Establishment**. **Who:** owners and managers edit; other roles view.

The page gathers the establishment's data in four tabs: **General**, **Languages**, **Public page** and **Prices
and VAT**. Tabs stay in memory as you move between them: unsaved changes are not lost, and the page warns you with
**Unsaved changes**.

## General

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Name** | The name shown on the public page, emails and documents. |
| **Public address** | The last part of the booking page URL, `/b/<address>`. Lowercase letters, digits and hyphens only. **Changing it breaks links already shared** (website, social media, printed QR codes). |
| **Time zone** | The establishment's time zone (usually `Europe/Rome`). Calendar dates, "today", policy cutoff times and the days of the daily takings follow it. |
| **Email**, **Phone** | Contacts shown to customers. |
| **Address**, **Town**, **Province**, **Postcode**, **Country (ISO code)** | The establishment's address (also used for the public page's structured data). |
| **Beach concession reference** | Optional, for your documents. |

The currency is the euro and cannot be changed.

## Languages

**Default language** and **Enabled languages** (Italian, English, German, French). Enabled languages are offered on
the public page and in emails; texts you have not translated fall back to the default language. In multilingual
fields (names of packs, zones, extras) missing languages are marked **missing**.

## Public page

Activation of **Online bookings enabled**, **Websites allowed to embed**, embed code and **Public page theme** with
preview. They are the same data as the [Public page and widget](/en/gestore/pagina-pubblica/) page, which explains
them in full.

## Prices and VAT

Values used by the pricing engine when a rule does not say otherwise.

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Half-day factor** | Half-day price = day × factor (for example `0.6`). Greater than 0 and at most 1. Used when there is no specific rule for the morning or afternoon slot. |
| **Total rounding** | **None**, **To the euro** or **To 50 cents**. Applies only to the final booking total. |
| **Default VAT** | Rate for packs and extras without their own rate. Check it with your accountant: Arenile contains no hard-coded rates. |

<Aside type="note" title="Prices include VAT">
  All prices in Arenile include VAT. The split by rate is computed only for documents, cash desk and daily takings,
  to the cent.
</Aside>

## Several establishments

One installation can manage several establishments of the same organization, each with its own map, price lists,
policies, public page and users, with isolated data.

<Steps>

1. Open the **Establishment** switcher at the top of the sidebar.
2. Choose **+ New establishment** (only owners can create one).
3. Choose the **Organization**, enter the details and press **Create establishment**: the guided setup opens.

</Steps>

The **All establishments** page (at the bottom of the sidebar) lists the establishments you can access, with public
address, default language and online booking status, and the link to open each public page.

## Frequently asked questions

**Two people edit the settings at the same time: what happens?**
Saving checks the version: if a colleague saved first, Arenile does not overwrite their changes and offers
**Reload the latest data**.

**Can I change the time zone once the season has started?**
You can, but "today" and cutoff times change accordingly: do it only if the time zone was wrong.

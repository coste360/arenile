**Where:** Operations → **Map** → **Find a spot**, or from the **New booking** sheet. **Who:** whoever
creates bookings (owner, manager, cashier).

In August it happens that no spot is free for the whole week the customer asks for: the 15th is full, but one spot
is free from the 10th to the 14th and another one on the 15th and 16th. **Find a spot** looks for these combinations
and proposes the week on two spots, already priced.

<Screenshot name="admin-find-spot" alt="Find spot: a week searched with one spot change allowed, and the proposals" />

## Searching

<Steps>

1. Choose the **Dates** and the **Slot** (full day, morning or afternoon).
2. Set how many **Spots** you need: for a group the proposals prefer spots close to each other.
3. Choose the **spot changes allowed**: with **None** only spots free for the whole stay are searched; with 1, 2 or
   3 the same booking may move from one spot to another during the stay.
4. Optionally restrict the **Zones**, choose the **Pack** (needed to see the price and book) and the customer's
   **Agreement**.
5. **Search**.

</Steps>

Proposals are ranked: fewest spot changes first, then those staying longest on the same spot and the closest
ones; for groups, adjacent spots count too. Each proposal shows its **segments** (for example *B11 from 10/08 to
14/08, B12 from 15/08 to 16/08*) and the **total**, with the agreement applied if you chose one. From the map,
**Show on the map** highlights the spots of the proposal.

## Booking a proposal

Press **Book**, choose the customer and confirm. The booking has **one single line** occupying the spots of the
segments, each on its own days: one code, one balance and one manage link.

- **Price:** each day is priced with the category of the spot occupied that day. If the proposal moves from a
  front row to a second row, the days in the second row cost as the second row.
- **No surprises:** if a colleague sold one of the spots meanwhile, the booking is not created and the search runs
  again by itself; if the price changed the panel says so before confirming.

<Aside type="note" title="No overbooking">
Find a spot never sells a spot that is already taken: each segment is a normal allocation, checked by the database
like any other (no half day can be sold twice).
</Aside>

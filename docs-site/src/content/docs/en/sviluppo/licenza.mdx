## What does not require a license

- **Embedding the widget or linking** the booking page in a website or app, for example as the agency building the
  lido's website, as long as you do not install, host, configure, customise, maintain or support Arenile.
- **The operator's employees** installing and running Arenile on the operator's behalf.
- **Helping an operator free of charge**, unless that help is part of, or promotes, a paid offering relating to
  Arenile or to the management, booking or website of the establishment.
- **Renting a server** on which the operator installs Arenile themselves, including through a generic or one-click
  installation template, as long as the provider does not run Arenile for them and does not market an offering
  dedicated to Arenile.

## Conversion to AGPL-3.0

Each version of Arenile automatically switches to the **GNU AGPL-3.0-or-later** license three years after its first
public release (the BSL *Change Date*). Today's code will therefore be available under the AGPL in three years,
version by version.

## Why this choice

The goal is that Arenile stays **free for operators** and that nobody can take it and resell it as a paid service to
the very establishments it is given to for free. The BSL protects this use while keeping the code readable, modifiable
and open to contributions. The alternatives considered and the reasons are in the repository's ADR 0007.

The managed hosting **Arenile Pro** is a separate, future commercial product of Coste360: the core stays complete and
free for those who run their own establishments, and no feature needed to run a beach is reserved to the commercial
product.

## Contributions

Contributions require signing the CLA (see [Contributing](/en/sviluppo/contribuire/#cla)). New dependencies must have
a license compatible with both the BSL 1.1 and the AGPL-3.0 (for example MIT, BSD, Apache-2.0, ISC).

Arenile is installed and the first user exists. Now open the panel and take the establishment from "empty" to
"ready to sell". The design goal is clear: **a lido with 8 rows of 15 spots is configured in under 15 minutes**
starting from a template.

## 1. Sign in to the panel

Open `https://<your-domain>/admin/` and enter email and password.

<Screenshot name="admin-login" alt="Sign-in page of the Arenile panel" />

- If **two-step verification** is enabled for your user, after the password you are asked for the 6-digit
  **Verification code** from your authenticator app.
- After too many wrong attempts sign-in is blocked for a few minutes: it protects against password guessing.

## 2. The first establishment

The user created at installation belongs to an organization (the value of `--org` or
`ARENILE_BOOTSTRAP_OWNER_ORG`). From the **Establishment** switcher at the top of the sidebar choose
**+ New establishment** to create one: the guided setup opens right after creation.

<Aside type="tip">
  You can have several establishments in the same installation: each has its own map, price lists, policies,
  public page and users. The **All establishments** page lists them with their public address and online booking
  status.
</Aside>

## 3. The guided setup

It is also in the menu under **Configuration → Guided setup**, reserved to owners and managers. There are six
steps: **each one can be skipped** and everything can be changed later from the Configuration menu items.

<Screenshot name="admin-setup-wizard" alt="Arenile guided setup with its six steps" />

<Steps>

1. **Details and languages.** Name, public address (the last part of the URL `/b/<address>`), contacts, time zone,
   default language and enabled languages. They appear on the public page and in emails to customers.

2. **Template.** Pick the template closest to your establishment. It creates season, zones, catalogue and rate
   periods that you can change:

   | Template | What it creates |
   |---|---|
   | **Classic lido with rows** | Umbrellas in numbered rows priced by row and period (front and second row priced separately), a row of gazebos subject to approval, cabins and parking. The most common case. |
   | **Equipped beach by quantity** | No map: customers choose how many umbrellas and sunbeds, availability is a counter. |
   | **Lido with pool** | Rows on the beach plus a pool area with sunbeds. |
   | **Empty** | Nothing prefilled: you configure everything by hand. |

3. **Map rows.** Set the number of **Rows** and **Spots per row** and the opening and closing dates of the **First
   season**, then press **Create season and map**. The spots are created on the map with automatic numbering
   (row A closest to the sea). Templates without a map create zones and quantity equipment instead.

4. **Quick price list.** A table with the daily price per **category** (row) and **period** of the season (low,
   mid, high, peak). Amounts include VAT; leave the cells you do not need empty. **Generate price rules** creates
   the base rules automatically. Half days and long formulas are fine-tuned later in [Prices](/en/gestore/prezzi/).

5. **Public page.** Turn on online bookings, choose the colours and copy the code to paste into your website.
   Details in [Public page and widget](/en/gestore/pagina-pubblica/).

6. **Payments.** Choose how you collect payments: **Manual payment** (collected at the desk: cash, card, bank
   transfer; online bookings are paid on site) or **Stripe** for online payment, completed in
   [Online payments with Stripe](/en/gestore/pagamenti-online/).

</Steps>

At the end press **Finish setup**: the summary lists the skipped steps and offers the shortcuts **Edit the map**,
**Review prices** and **Establishment settings**. You can always use **Reopen the guided setup**.

## 4. Check before opening to the public

- [ ] The [coverage check](/en/gestore/prezzi/#coverage-check) reports no days without a price.
- [ ] The [simulator](/en/gestore/prezzi/#price-simulator) gives the prices you expect for an August day and a June day.
- [ ] The payment and cancellation [policies](/en/gestore/policy/) are the ones you want to communicate to customers.
- [ ] You have published the [booking conditions](/en/gestore/pagina-pubblica/#terms-and-privacy-notice).
- [ ] You sent a [test email](/en/gestore/email/) and received it.
- [ ] With Stripe in test mode you completed a trial booking from your phone.
- [ ] You created the staff [users](/en/gestore/team-e-ruoli/) with the right role.
- [ ] You have scheduled the [backups](/en/installazione/backup-e-ripristino/).

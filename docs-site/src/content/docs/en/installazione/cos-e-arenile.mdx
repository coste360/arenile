**Arenile** ([arenile.app](https://arenile.app)) is the management system for beach establishments (*stabilimenti balneari*), lidos, equipped beaches
and pools developed by [Coste360 SRL](https://www.coste360.it/). It replaces notebooks, spreadsheets and closed
software with a single program the operator installs on **their own** server: the data stays theirs and nobody
takes a commission on bookings.

## What it does

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Beach map" icon="puzzle">
    Editor with the Coste360 map kit (over a hundred illustrations: umbrellas, gazebos, walkways, services, sea
    condition flag), row generator, operational view of the day with colours and symbols per status, updated in
    real time on every workstation.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bookings and cash desk" icon="list-format">
    Bookings from the panel and online, planning calendar, customers, season passes with day releases, cash desk
    with opening and closing, daily takings for the accountant.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Prices and rules" icon="setting">
    Pricing engine configurable without code: rate periods, holidays, rows, fixed durations, promo codes,
    agreements. Policies for lead time, deposit, cancellation and approval. Price simulator, matrix and coverage
    check.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Public page and payments" icon="laptop">
    A booking page for smartphones, embeddable in the establishment's website with one line of code, in your
    colours. Online payments with **your** Stripe account, confirmation email with QR code and calendar file,
    customer manage area via magic link.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

### Guarantees that are not up for debate

- **No double selling.** Two customers can never take the same spot in the same half-day, not even when one books
  online and the other at the desk at the same instant: a database constraint prevents it, not a check in the
  program.
- **Confirmation only comes from the payment.** An online booking becomes confirmed only when the payment provider
  says so with a signed message, never because the customer's browser "says" it paid.
- **Verifiable prices.** Every booking keeps the full price breakdown and the accepted terms: a refund can always
  be reconstructed, even if the price list has changed since.
- **Every change leaves a trace.** Every status change of a booking writes the activity log and the matching event
  in the same operation.

## Who it is for

| Type of establishment | How Arenile handles it |
|---|---|
| Classic lido with numbered rows of umbrellas | **Chosen spot** mode: the customer picks the umbrella on the map |
| Equipped beach without fixed places | **Quantity** mode: you sell a number of umbrellas and sunbeds |
| Premium areas assigned by staff | **Zone** mode: the customer picks the zone, the spot is assigned later (even automatically) |
| Lido with pool, beach club, gazebos and cabanas | Modes combine within the same establishment |
| Several lidos of the same company | Several establishments in one installation, with isolated data |

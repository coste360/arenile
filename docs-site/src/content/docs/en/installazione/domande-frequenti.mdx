## Is there a free management system for beach establishments?

Yes, Arenile is a free, source-available management system for beach establishments that you install on your own
server, with no fees and no paid licenses. It includes the umbrella map, online umbrella booking, price lists, cash
desk and season passes.

## Is Arenile really free?

Yes, for those who run their own establishment. Arenile is licensed under BSL 1.1: if you use it for the beaches you
own or run directly there are no fees, paid editions or limits on umbrellas, bookings or users. The only cost is
hosting (a VPS for a few euros a month or a computer you already have). A commercial license is needed only to offer
Arenile to third parties as a service. Details in [License](/en/sviluppo/licenza/).

## I am an agency or a technician: can I install it for my clients?

Not without a commercial license. The free license only covers the establishments you own or run directly.
Installing, configuring, customising, hosting it (as a hosted service or SaaS), maintaining or supporting Arenile on behalf of other
establishments for a fee, or reselling it, requires a commercial license from Coste360 SRL: write to
**info@coste360.it**. If you are an operator, your employees can install Arenile for you, or you can install it
yourself on a server you rent.

If you build the beach's website you can add the widget or the link to the booking page without a license, as long as
you do not install, host, configure or support Arenile. Helping an operator install it for free is allowed only if it
is not part of, and does not promote, a paid offer tied to Arenile or to the running, bookings or website of the
establishment.

## We are a consortium of beaches: can we use it for our members?

Each member beach can install and use Arenile for free for itself. A consortium or association that does not control
its member beaches does not run them directly, so installing, hosting or supporting Arenile for members requires a
commercial license from Coste360 SRL: write to **info@coste360.it**.

## Why BSL instead of an open source license?

Because we want Arenile to stay free for operators and nobody to be able to take it and resell it as a paid service to
the same establishments we give it to for free. The Business Source License 1.1 protects this free use from resellers,
while keeping the code readable, modifiable and open to contributions. Each version then moves to
**AGPL-3.0 or later** three years after its first public release.

## Are there commissions on online bookings?

No: Arenile charges no booking commissions. Unlike commission-based booking portals, online payments go through your
own Stripe account and the money goes straight to you: you only pay the payment network's fees, no percentage to the
platform. You can also leave online payments off and collect everything at the cash desk. See
[Online payments](/en/gestore/pagamenti-online/).

## Can I use it with my WordPress site?

Yes. The umbrella booking widget goes into a WordPress "Custom HTML" block, or into Wix, Joomla, Squarespace or any
website, with a one-line script or an iframe. You can also share the direct link to the booking page on social media
or Google Business. See [Embed the widget in your website](/en/gestore/pagina-pubblica/#embed-the-widget-in-your-website).

## Do I need a fiscal printer (registratore telematico)?

Arenile **is not a fiscal printer** and does not send daily takings to the Italian Revenue Agency: the commercial
document is issued with your own device, while Arenile handles the cash desk, payments and the export of daily
takings for your accountant. Receipts generated by Arenile are non-fiscal. Compliance of the configuration (VAT rates,
document issuing, retention) remains the responsibility of the operator and their advisor. See
[Daily takings](/en/gestore/corrispettivi/).

## Does it work without internet?

The server can also run on a local network (for example the [desktop app](/en/gestore/app-desktop/), a mini PC or a
Raspberry Pi at the beach), but online bookings need it to be reachable from the internet. If the connection drops the
panel still lets you check the map of the day and record check-ins, notes and cash payments, which are synchronised
when the network is back. New bookings need the connection, to guarantee there are no double sales. See
[Offline use](/en/gestore/uso-offline/).

## What is the difference between a beach management system and a booking portal?

An umbrella booking portal sells your spots on its own website and keeps a percentage of every booking. A beach
management system like Arenile is the software you run the establishment with (beach, price lists, cash desk,
customers) and that publishes online umbrella booking on your own website: bookings carry no commission and the
relationship with the customer stays yours.

## Can I manage several beaches?

Yes. A single installation runs several beach establishments, each with its own map, price lists, policies and booking
page. For many establishments [PostgreSQL](/en/installazione/database/) is recommended.

## Which kinds of establishment does it support?

Beaches with umbrellas in rows and numbered spots, quantity-based equipped beaches (e.g. loose sunbeds), zones where
the operator assigns the spot, gazebos, tents, palm umbrellas and pool areas. The modes can be combined in the same
establishment. See [Zones](/en/gestore/zone/).

## Is my customers' data safe and GDPR compliant?

The data stays on the server you choose: nobody, not even Coste360, can access it. Arenile collects only the data it
needs, versions consents, lets you export and anonymise a customer's data and never logs emails or phone numbers in
clear. The operator remains the data controller. See [Privacy](/en/installazione/privacy/).

This page helps operators use Arenile in compliance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and the Italian personal
data protection code.

<Aside type="caution">
  It is **informational** and is not legal advice. The privacy notice template must be adapted to your business
  and, where possible, checked with a privacy consultant or your DPO. Compliance of the processing remains the
  controller's responsibility.
</Aside>

## Who does what

| Party | GDPR role | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Establishment operator** | **Controller** | Decides purposes and means, answers to data subjects. |
| **Whoever hosts the service** (VPS provider, IT person who runs the server for the operator) | **Processor** (art. 28) | A data processing agreement (DPA) is needed. |
| **Stripe** (if you enable online payments) | Per Stripe's terms | Check the data processing agreement in your Stripe account. |
| **SMTP provider** (if you send emails) | Processor | Check the provider's DPA. |
| **Coste360 SRL** | No role in a self-managed installation with push notifications off | Develops the software but **neither receives nor processes** the data of your installations. No telemetry by default. |
| **Coste360 SRL**, for the push notification relay (only if you turn it on) | **Data processor** (art. 28), with Apple and Google as sub-processors | The relay receives the push token of the staff phone, the language and which generic alert to show, never customer data. The token is a persistent device identifier, so it is **pseudonymous** personal data, not anonymous. A data processing agreement (DPA) with Coste360 is needed. |

## What data Arenile processes

| Category | Data | Purpose | Legal basis (indicative) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Online booking | First name, last name, email, phone (optional if you decide so) | Manage the booking, send confirmation and manage link | Performance of a contract |
| Desk customers and season pass holders | As above, plus internal notes and history | Managing the relationship and the passes | Performance of a contract |
| Payments | Amounts, method, transaction references (card data stays with Stripe) | Collection, refunds, accounting | Contract and legal obligation |
| Marketing | Separate and optional consent, with date, source and text version | Offers and news | Consent |
| Panel users | Email, role, sign-ins, activity log | Security and traceability | Legitimate interest |
| Technical data | IP for request limits, logs without emails and phone numbers in clear | Service security | Legitimate interest |

Arenile **does not require** special categories of data (art. 9). Avoid writing health information in notes; if
needed for accessibility, keep it to the minimum (for example "accessible spot").

## How Arenile helps

- **Minimisation**: first name, last name and email are enough to book online; the phone can be required or not.
  No customer account.
- **Versioned terms and privacy notice**: each published version is immutable and each booking stores the accepted
  version and the moment of acceptance.
- **Separate marketing consent**, optional, never preselected, with date, source and text version.
- **Export** of a customer's data as JSON from their record (right of access and portability).
- **Anonymisation** on request: name, contacts, notes and consents replaced for good, while bookings and payments
  stay for accounting obligations (art. 17.3).
- **Protected recipients**: the addresses of sent emails are stored encrypted and masked in the log.
- **No third-party cookies** in the widget and no tracking by default.

<Aside type="note">
  Automatic anonymisation of customers without bookings for N years (default 5) is foreseen by the specification
  but not available yet: until then anonymise by hand from the customer record.
</Aside>

### Data imports

The CSV file uploaded in **Import data** (names, emails, phone numbers) stays in the database only as long as it is
needed: it is deleted as soon as the import is applied, failed or discarded, and after 7 days anyway when it is
never applied. Until then its contents are not covered by the anonymisation of a single customer nor by the export
of their data: discard the files you will not use.

### Update check

To show the new version notice, every installation (server and desktop app) reads a public file from the Coste360
server once a day. The request contains no data of customers or of the beach, no cookie and no identifier: it
reveals only the version, edition and operating system (User-Agent) and, like any HTTP request, the IP address of
the installation. Coste360 uses this information only for aggregate counts. Turn it off with
`ARENILE_UPDATE_CHECK=false` or from the desktop app settings (see
[Upgrades](/en/installazione/aggiornamenti/#new-version-notice)).

## What you must do

<Steps>

1. **Publish the privacy notice** and the conditions on the public page (**Configuration → Public page**,
   conditions section) and bump the version at every change.
2. **Appoint the processors** (hosting, SMTP, any support provider) with an art. 28 agreement.
3. **Keep the record of processing activities** (art. 30) if required: it is good practice anyway.
4. **Assign panel roles** on a least-privilege basis (beach staff, cashier, read only).
5. **Protect the backups**: they contain personal data.
6. **Handle data subject requests** within one month with export and anonymisation (see
   [Customers and GDPR](/en/gestore/clienti/)).
7. **Breaches**: consider notifying the supervisory authority within 72 hours (art. 33).
8. If you add analytics or advertising pixels to your website, handle cookie consent on the website: they are
   outside Arenile.

</Steps>

## Privacy notice template

Use this outline as a basis for the notice under arts. 13 and 14 GDPR. Replace the parts in square brackets.

```markdown
# Privacy notice — [Establishment name]

## 1. Controller
[Company name], [address], VAT no. [...], email [...], certified email [...].
[DPO, if any: name and contacts.]

## 2. Data processed
First name, last name, email and/or phone; booking data (dates, spot,
services); payment data (amounts, outcome, transaction references: card data
is processed directly by [Stripe]); marketing consent, if given.

## 3. Purposes and legal bases
a) managing the booking and the service — performance of a contract;
b) tax and accounting obligations — legal obligation;
c) service communications (confirmations, reminders) — performance of a contract;
d) offers and newsletters — consent, optional and revocable;
e) security of the booking system — legitimate interest.

## 4. Whether providing data is mandatory
Data for purposes a)–c) is needed to book; consent for purpose d) is optional
and refusing it has no consequence on the booking.

## 5. Recipients
Hosting provider [name], email provider [name], payment provider [Stripe],
accountant, authorities where required by law. Processors are appointed under
art. 28 GDPR.

## 6. Transfers outside the EU
[State whether any, and the safeguards adopted.]

## 7. Retention
Booking data: for the duration of the relationship and then for [N] years;
accounting documents: for the period required by law; marketing data: until
consent is withdrawn or for [N] months from the last contact.

## 8. Data subject rights
Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection,
withdrawal of consent at any time, by writing to [email]. Right to lodge a
complaint with the supervisory authority.

## 9. Version
Version [x], updated on [date].
```

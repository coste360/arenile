Each beach has **a single server**, holding the archive (bookings, customers, takings). The options differ in
**where that server runs**; staff devices (desk PC, tablets, phones) all connect the same way, from the browser.

## The four options

| Where the server runs | Internet | Online booking | Status | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Online**: VPS, hosting or Docker | required | yes | <Badge text="Available" variant="success" /> | The standard option, recommended for almost every beach. |
| **On-site server**: mini PC, NAS or Raspberry Pi at the beach, with Docker | optional | only if exposed to the internet (domain or tunnel) | <Badge text="Available" variant="success" /> | Same image as the online installation. |
| **Local desktop**: app on the desk PC | not required | no | <Badge text="Coming (M6)" variant="caution" /> | For those who work mainly at the desk, with a local network mode for other devices. |
| **Hosting managed by Coste360 (Arenile Pro)** | required | yes | <Badge text="Future" variant="note" /> | Commercial offering for those who do not want to manage a server. |

<CardGrid>
  <Card title="Online (recommended)" icon="rocket">
    A VPS costing a few euros a month, with Docker. The beach is reachable from anywhere, the booking page works
    around the clock and Stripe payments arrive even at night. You need a domain (for example
    `prenota.mio-lido.it`).
  </Card>
  <Card title="On-site server" icon="server">
    A mini PC or Raspberry Pi at the beach, with the same Docker image. It keeps working when the internet drops,
    but for online booking it must be reachable from outside (a domain with open ports, or a tunnel). Think about
    a UPS and off-site backups.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Local desktop (coming)" icon="laptop">
    A file to download and a double click on the desk PC: no server, no terminal. No internet, therefore no public
    page or online payments. Packages for Windows, macOS and Linux will be downloadable from the Coste360 portal
    after signing in.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Arenile Pro (future)" icon="star">
    Hosting managed by Coste360 for those who do not want to deal with servers, upgrades and backups. A separate,
    commercial product; the core stays complete and free for those who run their own establishments.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>

## How to choose

1. **Do you want online bookings?** You need a server reachable from the internet: choose **Online** (or an
   **On-site server** exposed through a domain or tunnel).
2. **Do you already have a VPS, or someone who can manage one?** Online with Docker is the simplest and most
   robust path.
3. **Unreliable connection at the beach and mostly desk work?** On-site server today, desktop edition when it is
   available.
4. **You do not want to deal with anything?** Consider managed hosting when it becomes available.

<Aside type="tip" title="Minimum requirements">
  1 vCPU and 512 MB of RAM are enough for one beach; 1 GB of disk plus room for backups. Linux `amd64` or `arm64`
  (including Raspberry Pi 4 or 5 with a 64-bit system).
</Aside>

## Clients

Valid for every option:

- **the browser**: web panel, nothing to install, on PC, tablet and phone;
- **the desktop app in remote mode** <Badge text="Coming (M6)" variant="caution" />: dedicated window and bridge
  to the local receipt printer and fiscal register;
- **the mobile app for staff** <Badge text="Coming (M7)" variant="caution" />: map of the day, QR check-in,
  payments, even offline.

## What is excluded, and why

There are no hybrid models with **two synchronised servers** (for example offline desktop plus VPS). Two
synchronising archives would have to resolve conflicts between bookings, and the guarantee that a spot is never
sold twice could no longer be absolute. To move from one option to another you use **backup and restore**: the
SQLite backup is the same file in every edition.

<Aside type="note" title="Where to get what">
  - **GitHub**: source code and build instructions. No prebuilt binaries are published on GitHub.
  - **GitHub Container Registry**: the Docker image `ghcr.io/coste360/arenile`, public and free.
  - **Coste360 portal**: ready-made desktop packages (Windows, macOS, Linux), after signing in, once available.
</Aside>

Arenile follows semantic versioning; the full list of changes, item by item, is in the repository's
`CHANGELOG.md`. This page tells the news from the point of view of whoever uses the program.

<Aside type="tip">
  **Arenile 1.0** is the first stable version and includes every milestone below. Upgrade notes apply from here on.
</Aside>

## 1.0 — First stable version

- **[Daily sale and tickets](/en/gestore/vendita-giornaliera/)** numbered per day, with
  [spot assignment](/en/gestore/assegnazione-ombrelloni/) by tap or automatic and
  [entry tickets](/en/gestore/biglietti-d-ingresso/) checked at the gate with a QR code.
- **[ESC/POS printers](/en/gestore/stampanti/)** over Wi-Fi or Ethernet from the desktop app: tickets, receipts and
  the Z closing.
- **[Customer emails](/en/gestore/email/)** with editable texts in four languages, preview and test sending, and
  **automatic reminders** for the balance and the day before.
- **[New map kit](/en/gestore/mappe-ed-editor/)** "Modern", with the umbrella coloured by its status; the "Classic"
  kit stays available.
- **[Accessibility](/en/gestore/accessibilita/)**: online booking at WCAG 2.1 level AA, usable with the keyboard
  alone and readable by screen readers.
- Multiple cash registers, test mode, guaranteed refunds, Excel exports and CSV import.
- The customer's phone number is optional everywhere.

## M5 — Completing the season

- Guided **[season rollover](/en/gestore/cambio-stagione/)**: rate periods, closures, prices and policies copied to
  the new season with a preview.
- Season pass **[renewals](/en/gestore/rinnovi/)** with right of pre-emption on the same spot, proposal by email and
  acceptance at the desk or online.
- **[Waitlist](/en/gestore/lista-d-attesa/)** with priority and automatic offers when a spot frees up.
- **[Customer credits](/en/gestore/crediti/)** usable as a payment method, with ledger and expiry.
- Full **[reports](/en/gestore/report/)** with comparison with the previous year and CSV export.
- **[Installable panel](/en/gestore/uso-offline/)** that keeps working, in a limited way, without a connection.
- **[API keys and webhooks](/en/gestore/chiavi-api-e-webhook/)** configurable from the panel.
- Maps with the **sea at the top** by default and reorientation of the layout when you change the sea side.

**Upgrade notes:** new database migrations (applied automatically at start-up); new optional variable
`ARENILE_WEBHOOKS_ALLOW_PRIVATE` to deliver webhooks to addresses of the local network.

## M6 — Desktop edition

- **[Desktop app](/en/gestore/app-desktop/)** for Windows, macOS and Linux: its own window, system tray icon,
  automatic backups and restore, **local network mode** for the other devices of the beach and remote mode with
  fiscal bridge. Packages will be downloadable from the Coste360 portal <Badge text="Coming soon" variant="caution" />.
- **[Devices](/en/gestore/dispositivi/)**: QR code pairing, access limited to the establishment and revocation.

## M4 — Public page and online payments <Badge text="Public MVP" variant="success" />

- **Public booking page** at `/b/<address>`, built for smartphones, in the three modes (chosen spot on the map,
  zone, quantity), mixed too, in four steps: When, Where, What, Details and payment.
- **`embed.js` widget** (under 3 KB) and iframe to embed it in any website, with the establishment's **theme**
  (colours, font, corner radius, logo, texts) and allowed websites.
- **Online payments with Stripe Checkout** using the establishment's encrypted keys; confirmation only from the
  signed webhook, event de-duplication, automatic **reconciliation** every 15 minutes, **refunds** through Stripe.
- **Transactional emails** from your SMTP server: confirmation with QR code and `.ics` calendar file,
  cancellation, refund, manager notifications; delivery log and test email.
- **Customer manage area** via magic link: details and QR, balance payment, changes and cancellation within the
  rules, day releases for pass holders, request for a new link.
- **Versioned terms**: each booking stores the accepted version.
- **Automatic assignment** of the spot for zones in "Zone" mode.
- No double selling between desk and online, verified by concurrency tests under parallel load.
- This **documentation**, in Italian and English, with always-current screenshots and interactive components.

**Upgrade notes:** new database migration (applied automatically at startup); new optional variable
`ARENILE_MAIL_DIR`. For online payments configure Stripe and the webhook (see
[Online payments with Stripe](/en/gestore/pagamenti-online/)).

## Map kit

- The 107 original Coste360 illustrations replace dots and rectangles in the editor, the operational map and the
  booking mini-map.
- Palette with search and categories, illustration and flag colour in the properties, modular walkways that join
  up.
- Spot status is a ring around the drawing plus a symbol per status: readable without colours.

**Upgrade notes:** on SQLite the migration rebuilds the spots table (a few seconds); take a backup before
upgrading.

## M3 — Bookings from the panel and cash desk

- **Today** page, real-time operational **Map**, **Planning** calendar with drag and drop and keyboard.
- **Bookings** with price preview, changes, moves, cancellations with refund or credit per the accepted terms,
  check-in.
- **Customers** with GDPR export and anonymisation, **season passes** with day release and resale.
- **Cash desk** with opening, movements, closing with count and Z report; **daily takings** in CSV and JSON.

## M2 — Prices and policies

- Price rules, fixed-duration prices, promo codes, agreements.
- Booking policies: lead time, duration, payment, cancellation, approval.
- Simulator, price matrix, coverage check, season copy.

## M1 — Inventory and map

- Multiple establishments, seasons, rate periods, closures, users and roles.
- Map editor with row generator, zones, spots, decorations, pools; catalogue.
- Guided setup with four templates and a quick price list; demo data.

## M0 — Foundations

- Single binary, Docker image, SQLite or PostgreSQL, automatic migrations, CLI.
- Sign-in with optional two-step verification, roles, activity log, events.
